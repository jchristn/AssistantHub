2025 Pay Review and Bonus Announcement

From: Priya Raman, Chief Executive Officer. To: all Meridian Instruments employees. Date: 28 April 2025.

Thank you for FY2024/25

FY2024/25 was a year of solid progress. Halcyon gas-detector shipments grew across all three models, the Tessera platform added its largest TesseraCloud customer to date, and the Lumen LF-60P launch in Rotterdam landed on schedule. Group revenue finished slightly below our stretch plan, largely because of component shortages in the first half, but operating margin was ahead of target. Thank you for everything you did to deliver that.

Meridian Incentive Plan payout

The Board has approved a Meridian Incentive Plan (MIP) payout factor of 92% of target for FY2024/25. Your bonus is your MIP target percentage for your grade, multiplied by 92%, applied to the base salary you earned during FY2024/25. The MIP bonus will be paid with the June 2025 payroll at every site.

MIP target percentages by grade band are published in the Grade Structure, Salary Ranges and Bonus Targets 2025 document on Keel.

2025 salary review budgets

After reviewing market data and inflation at each site, the Executive Committee has approved the following salary review budgets. Budgets are a percentage of the total base salary of eligible employees in each country; individual increases will vary with performance and position in range.

What happens next

  • Managers will receive their budgets in Keel when the compensation module opens on 6 May 2025.
  • Deadlines for proposals and calibration differ by grade band and are set out in the Salary Review Timeline FY2025/26 by Grade Band.
  • All increases are effective 1 July 2025.
  • You will receive your salary review letter in Keel in June 2025.
