# Budget Holder Guide

Owner: Financial Planning and Analysis (FP&A), Finance. Effective: 1 April 2025 (FY2025/26). Contact: fpa@meridian-instruments.example.

## 1. Who this guide is for

A budget holder is the person accountable for the income and costs recorded against one or more cost centres in Atlas. At Meridian Instruments most budget holders are department heads or team leads at grade G6 or above, but any employee can be appointed budget holder by their Head of Department with FP&A's agreement. Budget holder assignments are listed against each code in the Cost Centre Directory.

This guide explains what budget holders are responsible for, the monthly and quarterly deadlines, and where to find reports. It does not set approval limits; those are in the Delegation of Authority and Approval Matrix (POL-FIN-003).

## 2. Responsibilities

As a budget holder you are expected to:

- understand the annual budget for your cost centres and the assumptions behind it;
- approve spend only where budget exists or where an overspend has been agreed with FP&A;
- review your budget report every month and explain significant variances;
- keep headcount in your cost centres consistent with the approved plan, raising new roles through Keel > Team > Requisition to Hire;
- approve expense claims and purchase requisitions routed to you within the limits delegated to you under POL-FIN-003;
- make sure costs are coded to the right cost centre, and ask Finance to correct miscoding promptly;
- nominate a deputy in Atlas when you are on leave for more than three working days.

You remain accountable for approvals given by your nominated deputy.

## 3. The annual budget cycle

The fiscal year runs from 1 April to 31 March. The budget for FY2026/27 will follow this outline:

| Stage | Timing |
|---|---|
| Planning assumptions issued by FP&A | Early November |
| Draft cost centre budgets submitted | Mid December |
| Department reviews with CFO | January |
| Board approval | March |
| Budgets loaded into Atlas | Before 1 April |

Pay review costs are built into the budget centrally by Reward using the country budgets announced each year, so budget holders do not need to estimate salary increases themselves.

## 4. Month-end deadlines

Finance closes the books each month. Budget holders have two key deadlines:

1. Accruals. Tell Finance about any goods or services received but not yet invoiced by working day 2, using the accrual request in Atlas.
2. Variance commentary. Submit commentary on your cost centres by working day 5 of the following month.

Variance commentary is required for any line where actual spend differs from budget by more than 10% and more than £5,000 in the month, or for any cost centre whose year-to-date position is more than 5% overspent. Commentary should say what caused the variance, whether it is timing or permanent, and what action you are taking.

Budget reports are available in Atlas at Atlas > Finance > Budget Reports from working day 4. Reports show actuals, budget, latest forecast and commitments (open purchase orders) by cost centre and account.

## 5. Quarterly reforecast

FP&A runs a reforecast at the end of each quarter. Budget holders update their full-year forecast in Atlas.

| Quarter end | Reforecast window |
|---|---|
| Q1 (30 June) | Working days 6 to 10 of July |
| Q2 (30 September) | Working days 6 to 10 of October |
| Q3 (31 December) | Working days 6 to 10 of January |

FP&A reviews each department's reforecast with the Head of Department in the week after the window closes, and the consolidated forecast is presented to the CFO before the quarterly Board meeting. There is no Q4 reforecast; the year-end outturn is prepared by Finance. Forecasts should reflect known changes in headcount, committed purchase orders, planned projects and expected travel.

## 6. Approving spend

When an expense claim, card transaction or purchase requisition is routed to you, check that:

- the spend is for a genuine business purpose and complies with the relevant policy (Travel and Expense Policy POL-FIN-007, Corporate Card Policy POL-FIN-009 or the Procurement Manual POL-FIN-012);
- the correct cost centre and account are used;
- the amount is within your delegated limit under POL-FIN-003; if not, Atlas escalates it automatically;
- there is budget available, or an agreed overspend.

Do not approve your own spend or spend from which you personally benefit. Items that need approval above your limit must never be split into smaller amounts.

## 7. Capital expenditure

Capital items must be requested on form F-420 Capital Expenditure Request. The capex threshold and approval levels are set in POL-FIN-003. Capex is budgeted separately from operating costs and is reported on a separate Atlas report.

## 8. Recharges between cost centres

Costs shared between sites, for example Tessera engineering work done in Austin for a Rotterdam customer project, are recharged monthly using the recharge journal in Atlas. The sending and receiving budget holders must both approve the recharge before working day 3.

## 9. Year-end

At year end, FP&A issues specific instructions in February. Key points:

- raise purchase orders for goods and services due before 31 March in good time;
- budget does not carry over from one year to the next;
- prepayments for services spanning the year end must be flagged to Finance.

## 10. Support

FP&A business partners are assigned by department. Contact fpa@meridian-instruments.example for questions about budgets, forecasts and reports. For approval limits, see POL-FIN-003. For supplier and purchase order questions, contact procurement@meridian-instruments.example.
