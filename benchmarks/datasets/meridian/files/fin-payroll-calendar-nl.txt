1. HOW NL PAY WORKS

NL employees are paid monthly by SEPA transfer to the bank account held in Keel. The standard pay date is the 24th of each month. If the 24th falls on a weekend or a Dutch public holiday, pay is made on the previous working day. December pay is brought forward to Friday 19 December 2025 because of the Christmas period.

Payslips (loonstroken) are available in Keel > My Pay > Payslips two working days before each pay date.

2. CUT-OFF DATES

Change cut-off: changes that affect pay (new starters, leavers, bank details, salary changes, overtime and on-call claims, pension changes) must be fully approved by the 12th of the month to be included in that month's payroll. If the 12th is not a working day, the cut-off is the previous working day. Changes received after the cut-off are processed the following month.

Expense cut-off: expense claims approved in Atlas by the same change cut-off (the 12th) are reimbursed with that month's salary. Tax-free travel allowance (reiskostenvergoeding) is paid automatically in the month following the travel.

4. OTHER KEY PAYROLL DATES

- Holiday allowance (vakantiegeld), 8% of gross annual salary accrued June 2024 to May 2025: paid with the May 2025 payroll on Fri 23 May 2025.

- MIP bonus for FY2024/25: paid with the June 2025 payroll (Tue 24 Jun 2025).

- Salary review increases effective 1 July 2025: first paid in the July 2025 payroll (Thu 24 Jul 2025).

- Annual statement (jaaropgaaf) for calendar year 2025: available in Keel > My Pay > Tax Documents by 31 January 2026.

- Pension contributions to Zeevaart Pensioen are deducted every month; see the Netherlands Pension and 30% Ruling FAQ.

5. BANK DETAILS AND CORRECTIONS

Update bank details in Keel > My Profile > Bank Details before the change cut-off. If you believe you have been underpaid by more than EUR 120, contact payroll-nl@meridian-instruments.example; Payroll will make an off-cycle correction payment within five working days. Smaller corrections are made in the next scheduled payroll.
