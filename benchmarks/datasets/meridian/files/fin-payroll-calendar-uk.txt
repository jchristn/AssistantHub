1. HOW UK PAY WORKS

UK employees are paid monthly, in arrears for salary and allowances, by BACS transfer to the bank account held in Keel. The standard pay date is the 25th of each month. If the 25th falls on a weekend or a bank holiday, pay is made on the previous working day. December pay is brought forward to Friday 19 December 2025 because of the Christmas closure.

Payslips are available in Keel > My Pay > Payslips two working days before each pay date.

2. CUT-OFF DATES

Change cut-off: changes that affect pay (new starters, leavers, bank details, salary changes, overtime and on-call claims, pension contribution changes) must be fully approved by the 10th of the month to be included in that month's payroll. If the 10th is not a working day, the cut-off is the previous working day. Changes received after the cut-off are processed the following month.

Expense cut-off: expense claims approved in Atlas by the 12th of the month (or the previous working day) are reimbursed with that month's payroll. Claims approved after the expense cut-off are paid the following month.

5. BANK DETAILS AND CORRECTIONS

Update bank details in Keel > My Profile > Bank Details before the change cut-off. If you believe you have been underpaid by more than £100, contact payroll-uk@meridian-instruments.example; Payroll will make an off-cycle correction payment within five working days. Smaller corrections are made in the next scheduled payroll.
