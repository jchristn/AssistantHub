# Travel Booking Guide

Owner: Finance (Travel Desk). Effective: 7 April 2025. Contact: travel@meridian-instruments.example.

## 1. Overview

All business travel for Meridian Instruments employees is booked through Atlas using our travel management company, Northwind Travel Management. Booking centrally keeps costs within policy, lets us locate travellers in an emergency, and gives us better rates with airlines and hotels.

This guide explains how to book. The rules on what you may book, including class of travel, hotel caps per city and per-diem rates, are in the Travel and Expense Policy (POL-FIN-007). Approval limits for spend are in the Delegation of Authority and Approval Matrix (POL-FIN-003).

## 2. Before you book

1. Check that the trip is necessary and that a video call would not do.
2. For international travel, submit form F-221 International Travel Pre-Approval within the lead time set out in POL-FIN-007. The F-221 reference number must be entered when you book.
3. Make sure you know the cost centre the trip will be charged to.

## 3. How to book

1. Go to Atlas > Travel > Book Trip.
2. Enter your trip details, the cost centre and, for international trips, your F-221 reference.
3. Search for flights, rail and hotels. Options within policy are shown first and marked "In policy".
4. Select your options and submit. Atlas sends the booking to Northwind Travel Management, who ticket it and send an itinerary to your Meridian email.

Rail travel should be booked at least 7 days ahead where possible to secure advance fares. Flights should be booked at least 14 days ahead where possible.

For complex itineraries, group bookings or visa support, contact Northwind Travel Management directly on the 24/7 line +44 113 496 0735, quoting your Atlas trip number.

## 4. Out-of-policy bookings

If you select an option outside policy, such as a hotel above the city cap or a higher class of travel, Atlas asks you for a reason and routes the booking for approval. Out-of-policy bookings need budget holder approval in Atlas before they are ticketed. Where POL-FIN-007 requires a higher level of approval, for example for business class flights, Atlas routes the booking to that approver as well.

Bookings that are not approved within 24 hours may be cancelled by Northwind Travel Management if the fare cannot be held.

## 5. Hotels

Book hotels through Atlas so that our negotiated rates are applied. Preferred hotels near each Meridian site are shown at the top of the search results. If you have to book a hotel directly, for example because a customer has arranged accommodation, record it in Atlas as an "Off-system booking" so that the Travel Desk knows where you are.

## 6. Changes, cancellations and emergencies

- Change or cancel bookings through Atlas > Travel > Book Trip > My Trips, or by calling Northwind Travel Management.
- Unused non-refundable tickets are held as credits and must be used for future Meridian travel.
- In an emergency while travelling, call the Northwind Travel Management 24/7 line on +44 113 496 0735.

## 7. Paying for travel

Flights, rail and most hotels booked through Atlas are billed centrally to Meridian and do not need to be paid personally. Meals, local transport and other costs during the trip are paid by corporate card or personally and claimed through Atlas > Expenses > New Claim under POL-FIN-007.
