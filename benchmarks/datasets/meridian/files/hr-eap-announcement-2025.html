New Employee Assistance Programme Provider: Harbour Wellbeing

People Team announcement, 12 May 2025. From Adaeze Okafor, Chief People Officer.

What is changing

From 1 June 2025, Harbour Wellbeing becomes Meridian Instruments' Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) provider at all four sites: Leeds, Rotterdam, Austin and Penang. The previous EAP service ends at midnight on 31 May 2025. Anyone currently receiving counselling through the old service will be contacted directly so that their support continues without a break.

What the EAP offers

  • A free, confidential 24/7 helpline staffed by qualified counsellors.
  • Up to 8 counselling sessions per issue per year (previously 6), by phone, video or face to face.
  • Legal and financial information lines for everyday problems such as debt, housing and consumer issues.
  • Manager support line for advice on supporting a team member.
  • Online wellbeing library, webinars and self-help programmes.

Family members living in the same household as the employee are also eligible to use the service.

Confidentiality

The EAP is completely confidential. Meridian receives only anonymous usage statistics, such as the number of calls per quarter, and never learns who has used the service or why, unless there is a serious risk to someone's safety.

Why we changed provider

Feedback from last year's engagement survey told us that employees wanted faster access to counsellors, more sessions and better support outside the UK. Harbour Wellbeing offers counsellors who speak Dutch, Malay, Mandarin and Tamil as well as English, and typically arranges a first counselling session within three working days of the initial call.

Where to find more

A summary card with the helpline numbers will be placed in every site kitchen and on the Keel home page. The EAP sits alongside the other wellbeing benefits described in the Benefits Comparison by Country 2025. Questions about the change can be sent to benefits@meridian-instruments.example.
