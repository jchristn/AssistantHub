# Employee Referral Scheme

Policy code: POL-HR-044
Effective: 1 February 2025
Owner: Talent Acquisition (recruitment@meridian-instruments.example)

## 1. Purpose

Great colleagues often know other great people. The Employee Referral Scheme rewards Meridian employees who refer candidates who are hired into permanent roles.

## 2. How to refer

1. Find the vacancy on the internal jobs page in Keel.
2. Submit your referral through Keel > Recognition > Refer a Friend, attaching the candidate's CV and a short note on why they are a good fit.
3. Talent Acquisition confirms receipt and tells you whether the referral is accepted.

A referral is valid for 12 months from the date it is submitted. If the candidate is hired into any permanent role at Meridian within that period, the referral counts. Referrals must be made before the candidate applies directly or is introduced by an agency; the earliest record in the applicant tracking system wins.

## 3. Referral bonus amounts

The bonus depends on the site that employs the new hire and the grade of the role they are hired into.

| Site | G1–G5 hire | G6+ hire |
|---|---|---|
| UK | £1,500 | £3,000 |
| NL | €1,750 | €3,500 |
| US | $2,000 | $4,000 |
| MY | RM 3,000 | RM 6,000 |

## 4. When the bonus is paid

The bonus is paid in full after the referred hire successfully completes probation at their site. It is paid through the referring employee's normal payroll in the month after probation is confirmed and is subject to tax and social security. Probation periods differ by country; see the Probation Policy (POL-HR-011). The referring employee must still be employed, and not under notice, on the payment date.

## 5. Who is eligible

All permanent and fixed-term employees are eligible, except:

- the hiring manager for the role and anyone on the interview panel;
- employees in the People team and Talent Acquisition;
- members of the Executive Committee (G10).

Referrals of former employees who left less than 6 months ago, and of contractors or agency workers already working at Meridian, are not eligible. If two employees refer the same candidate, the first referral recorded in Keel receives the bonus.

## 6. Fairness

Referred candidates go through the same selection process as all other candidates, as set out in the Recruitment and Selection Manual (POL-HR-052). Referring someone does not guarantee an interview. Questions about the scheme should go to recruitment@meridian-instruments.example.
