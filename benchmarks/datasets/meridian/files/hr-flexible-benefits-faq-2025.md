# Flexible Benefits Enrolment FAQ 2025 (UK)

Owner: Reward, People Team. Published: 24 February 2025. Applies to: UK employees of Meridian Instruments Ltd. Contact: benefits@meridian-instruments.example.

## About the enrolment window

### When is the 2025 enrolment window?

The UK flexible benefits enrolment window runs from 3 March to 21 March 2025. Choices made in the window take effect on 1 April 2025, the start of FY2025/26, and run until 31 March 2026.

### Where do I make my choices?

Log in to Keel and go to Keel > Benefits > Enrol. Your current selections are shown, together with the cost of each option per month. Click "Submit choices" before the window closes; saved but unsubmitted changes are not processed.

### What happens if I do nothing?

Your current benefits roll over to the new year, except for bought or sold holiday, which resets to zero each year. If you want to buy or sell holiday for FY2025/26 you must make a new choice.

## Holiday trading

### How much holiday can I buy?

You can buy up to 5 days of additional annual leave. The cost is deducted from your salary in equal monthly instalments through salary sacrifice.

### Can I sell holiday?

Yes. You can sell up to 3 days of annual leave. You must still take at least the statutory minimum holiday in the year, and your remaining entitlement after the sale is shown in Keel before you submit.

### When can I use bought days?

Bought days are added to your balance on 1 April and can be booked in the normal way through Keel > Time Off > Request Leave. Bought days that are not used by the end of the leave year are refunded through payroll in April. They cannot be carried over.

## Cycle to work

### How much can I spend under the cycle to work scheme?

You can apply for a bike and safety equipment worth up to £2,500. The cost is recovered through salary sacrifice over 12 months, which saves income tax and National Insurance.

### Can I join the cycle scheme outside the enrolment window?

Yes. The cycle to work scheme is open all year and is not limited to the enrolment window. Apply through Keel > Benefits > Enrol and select "Cycle to Work".

## Other options

### Which other benefits can I choose?

The flexible benefits menu for 2025 includes dental insurance, annual health screening, partner and family cover on private medical insurance, additional life cover and critical illness cover. Core benefits such as pension, life assurance and private medical cover for yourself are provided automatically and are described in the Benefits Comparison by Country 2025.

### How do I change my pension contribution?

Pension contributions are not part of the enrolment window. They can be changed in any month through Keel > Benefits > Pension, as explained in the UK Pension Scheme Guide.

## Changes during the year

### Can I change my choices after the window closes?

Only if you have a qualifying life event, such as marriage or civil partnership, the birth or adoption of a child, divorce, or your partner losing their own cover. You must request the change within 30 days of the event by emailing benefits@meridian-instruments.example with supporting evidence.

### What happens to my benefits if I leave?

Salary sacrifice deductions stop from your leaving date. Any outstanding balance on the cycle to work scheme is deducted from final pay. Bought holiday is pro-rated and adjusted in final pay.
