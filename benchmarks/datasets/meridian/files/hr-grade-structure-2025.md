## 1. About the grade structure

Every role at Meridian Instruments is assigned to one of ten grades, G1 to G10. Grades are grouped into five grade bands, which are used for salary review timing, approval levels, notice periods and some allowances. Grades are the same at every site; salary ranges are set locally in the site currency. Your grade is shown in Keel under My Profile.

| Grade band | Grades | Typical roles |
|---|---|---|
| Associate | G1–G3 | Production operator, technician, administrator, graduate engineer |
| Professional | G4–G5 | Engineer, accountant, HR advisor, field sales engineer |
| Senior/Lead | G6–G7 | Senior engineer, team lead, product manager |
| Head/Director | G8–G9 | Head of department, site director, engineering director |
| Executive | G10 | Members of the Executive Committee (ExCo) |

## 6. Meridian Incentive Plan (MIP) bonus targets

All permanent employees are eligible for the Meridian Incentive Plan. The target bonus is a percentage of base salary earned during the financial year. The actual payout is the target multiplied by the company payout factor, which is set after the year-end results and announced with the annual pay review.

| Grade band | Grades | MIP target (% of base salary) |
|---|---|---|
| Associate | G1–G3 | 5% |
| Professional | G4–G5 | 8% |
| Senior/Lead | G6–G7 | 12% |
| Head/Director | G8–G9 | 20% |
| Executive | G10 | 35% |

Employees who join after 1 January in a financial year are not eligible for MIP for that year. Employees who have resigned or are under notice on the payment date do not receive a payout.

## 7. How ranges are used

- New hires are normally offered a salary between the minimum and the midpoint of the grade. Offers above the midpoint follow the approval rules in the Recruitment and Selection Manual (POL-HR-052).
- Salary review increases are calculated from the review budget and your position in range; see the Salary Review Timeline FY2025/26 by Grade Band.
- Grade-linked allowances, such as the UK car allowance, are set out in the relevant policy, for example the Company Car and Car Allowance Policy (POL-FIN-015).
- Ranges are reviewed every April against market data for each site.
