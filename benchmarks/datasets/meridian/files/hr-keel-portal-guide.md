# Keel HR Portal User Guide

Owner: People Operations
Last updated: 17 February 2025
Contact: peoplehelp@meridian-instruments.example

## 1. What Keel is

Keel is Meridian Instruments' HR portal. It holds your employee record, pay documents, leave balances, benefits and HR requests for all four sites. Finance tasks such as expense claims, corporate cards and purchasing are done in Atlas, not Keel, and IT requests such as laptops and access are raised in Beacon.

You sign in to Keel with your normal Meridian single sign-on account. If you cannot sign in or need a password reset, raise a ticket in Beacon; the People team cannot reset passwords. Keel is available on the web and in the Keel mobile app, which supports leave requests, sickness reporting and payslips.

## 2. Navigation basics

The Keel home page shows your leave balance, open tasks and recent documents. The main menu has the following areas: Time Off, My Pay, My Profile, Benefits, Requests, Learning, Compliance, Recognition, Team (managers only) and Claims (Penang only). Menu paths in this guide are written as Keel > Area > Item.

## 3. Time off and sickness

| Task | Menu path |
|---|---|
| Request annual leave, PTO or floating holiday | Keel > Time Off > Request Leave |
| Report sickness absence | Keel > Time Off > Report Sickness |

To request leave, choose Keel > Time Off > Request Leave, select the leave type, dates and whether any day is a half day, then submit. Your manager receives a notification and the request shows as Pending until approved. You can cancel a pending or approved request from the same screen, up to the day before the leave starts.

To report sickness, choose Keel > Time Off > Report Sickness on your first day of absence and enter the expected return date if known. Reporting in Keel does not replace speaking to your manager; the Sickness Absence Policy (POL-HR-017) explains what you must do. When you return, Keel asks you to confirm the end date and, where required, complete the self-certification form F-330 online.

## 4. Pay and personal details

| Task | Menu path |
|---|---|
| View and download payslips | Keel > My Pay > Payslips |
| Tax documents (P60, jaaropgaaf, W-2, EA form) | Keel > My Pay > Tax Documents |
| Change bank details | Keel > My Profile > Bank Details |

Payslips appear two working days before each pay date. Bank detail changes must be made before your site's payroll cut-off to take effect that month; see the payroll calendar for your site. For security, a change of bank details triggers a confirmation email and a code sent to your registered mobile number. If you did not make the change, contact peoplehelp@meridian-instruments.example and raise a Beacon security ticket immediately.

My Profile also shows your grade, home cost centre, manager and contracted hours. If any of these are wrong, contact the People team rather than your manager.

## 5. Benefits

| Task | Menu path |
|---|---|
| Enrol in or change benefits | Keel > Benefits > Enrol |
| Change pension contributions | Keel > Benefits > Pension |

Keel > Benefits > Enrol shows the benefits available at your site and your current choices. UK flexible benefits can only be changed during the annual March window or within 30 days of a life event. Pension contribution changes are made in Keel > Benefits > Pension; UK and NL changes take effect in the next payroll if made by the date given in the relevant pension guide. US 401(k) deferral elections are made with the plan recordkeeper, not in Keel.

## 6. Requests

| Task | Menu path | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Flexible working request | Keel > Requests > Flexible Working | F-315 |
| Work from abroad request | Keel > Requests > Work From Abroad | F-318 |
| Parental leave notification | Keel > Requests > Parental Leave | F-301 |

Each request opens the online version of the form. The request is routed to your manager and then to the People team. You can track progress under Requests > My Requests. Eligibility and notice periods are set out in the Remote and Hybrid Working Policy (POL-HR-022) and the Parental Leave Policy (POL-HR-031).

## 7. Learning, compliance and recognition

| Task | Menu path | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Request external training | Keel > Learning > Training Request | F-260 |
| Annual and ad-hoc declarations | Keel > Compliance > Declarations | F-150 |
| Refer a candidate | Keel > Recognition > Refer a Friend | n/a |

Training requests are routed according to the Learning and Development Policy (POL-HR-035). The Compliance area holds the conflict of interest declaration (F-150) and your mandatory training record. Refer a Friend lets you submit a candidate for an advertised vacancy under the Employee Referral Scheme (POL-HR-044); you can see the status of your referrals on the same page.

## 8. Manager tasks

| Task | Menu path |
|---|---|
| Complete probation reviews | Keel > Team > Probation Reviews |
| Request a new or replacement role | Keel > Team > Requisition to Hire |

Managers see a Team area with their direct reports' leave calendar, absence records and pending approvals. Keel > Team > Probation Reviews lists upcoming review dates and the review forms required by the Probation Policy (POL-HR-011). Keel > Team > Requisition to Hire starts the approval of a new or replacement role; the approval steps are described in the Recruitment and Selection Manual (POL-HR-052).

## 9. Penang medical claims

Penang employees have an additional menu, Keel > Claims > Medical, for reimbursement of outpatient medical costs at non-panel clinics. Upload the receipt and medical certificate within 30 days of the visit. The annual limit and panel clinic arrangements are explained in the Malaysia Statutory Contributions and Medical Benefits Guide. Claims for other sites are not made in Keel; UK, NL and US medical benefits are handled by the insurers.
