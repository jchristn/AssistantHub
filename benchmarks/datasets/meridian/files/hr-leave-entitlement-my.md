# Holiday and Leave Entitlement - Malaysia

Policy code: POL-HR-014-MY
Applies to: all employees employed by Meridian Instruments (Malaysia) Sdn. Bhd. at the Penang site
Effective date: 1 January 2025
Owner: People team, Penang (peoplehelp@meridian-instruments.example)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how much paid annual leave Penang employees receive, which public holidays Meridian observes in Malaysia, and how leave is booked, carried forward and paid. It is one of four regional leave policies. Colleagues in Leeds, Rotterdam and Austin should refer to POL-HR-014, POL-HR-014-NL and POL-HR-014-US respectively, which follow the same structure but contain different entitlements. Entitlements in this policy are above the minimums in the Employment Act 1955.

## 2. Leave year

The Malaysian leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December (calendar year). The 2025 leave year therefore runs from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025. Entitlement is refreshed in Keel on 1 January each year.

## 3. Annual leave entitlement

Full-time Penang employees receive paid annual leave according to length of continuous service, in addition to public holidays. The higher entitlement applies from the start of the leave year following the relevant service anniversary.

| Continuous service | Annual leave (days) |
|---|---|
| Under 2 years | 16 |
| 2 to 5 years | 18 |
| More than 5 years | 22 |

The maximum annual leave entitlement is 22 days plus public holidays. Shift employees in Manufacturing (CC-3100) and Quality Assurance (CC-3110) have leave recorded in shifts in Keel, with one shift equal to one day. Part-time employees receive a pro rata entitlement.

## 5. Booking leave

All annual leave is requested and approved in Keel via Keel > Time Off > Request Leave. Employees should give notice of at least twice the length of the leave requested, for example two weeks' notice for one week of leave. Managers should respond within five working days. Leave is not confirmed until it shows as approved in Keel, and travel should not be booked before approval.

Managers may decline requests where business needs require, for example during peak production of Halcyon and Lumen units or during customer audits. Shift employees must submit requests before the shift roster for that month is published. Where more than two members of a team of eight or fewer request the same week, requests are normally approved in the order received.

## 6. Carry-forward

Penang employees may carry forward up to 7 days of unused annual leave into the next leave year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March of the new leave year; any carried-forward days not used by 31 March are forfeited. Leave above the 7-day limit is forfeited at 31 December. Untaken leave is not paid in lieu except on leaving.

## 7. Buying additional leave

The Malaysian benefits programme does not include buying additional leave. Employees who need additional time off may request unpaid leave from their manager.

## 8. Joiners, leavers and part-time staff

Employees who join or leave during the leave year receive a pro rata entitlement for the months worked. On leaving, any accrued but untaken leave is paid in the final salary payment, and any leave taken in excess of accrued entitlement is deducted. Managers may ask leavers to use outstanding leave during their notice period.

## 9. Leave, sickness and family leave

Penang employees receive paid sick leave separate from annual leave, based on service: 14 days (under 2 years), 18 days (2 to 5 years) and 22 days (more than 5 years) per year. Where hospitalisation is necessary, employees receive up to 60 days of hospitalisation leave per year, which includes any sick leave taken in that year. Sick leave must be supported by a certificate from a registered medical practitioner or panel clinic and reported in line with the Sickness Absence Policy (POL-HR-017). Family leave entitlements are set out in the Parental Leave Policy (POL-HR-031), which applies alongside Malaysian statutory maternity and paternity leave.
