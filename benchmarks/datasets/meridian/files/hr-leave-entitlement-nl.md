# Holiday and Leave Entitlement - Netherlands

Policy code: POL-HR-014-NL
Applies to: all employees employed by Meridian Instruments B.V. in the Netherlands (Rotterdam site and NL home-based staff)
Effective date: 1 January 2025
Owner: People team, Rotterdam (peoplehelp@meridian-instruments.example)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how much paid annual leave NL employees receive, which public holidays Meridian observes in the Netherlands, and how leave is booked, carried over and paid. It is one of four regional leave policies. Colleagues in Leeds, Austin and Penang should refer to POL-HR-014, POL-HR-014-US and POL-HR-014-MY respectively, which follow the same structure but contain different entitlements.

## 2. Leave year

The NL leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December (calendar year). The 2025 leave year therefore runs from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025. Entitlement is refreshed in Keel on 1 January each year.

## 3. Annual leave entitlement

Full-time NL employees (38 hours per week) receive 26 days of paid annual leave per leave year, in addition to public holidays. Of these, 20 days are statutory days (wettelijke vakantiedagen) and 6 days are extra-statutory days (bovenwettelijke vakantiedagen). Entitlement increases with continuous service as shown below. Service increments are applied from the start of the leave year following the relevant service anniversary and are treated as extra-statutory days.

| Continuous service | Annual leave (days) |
|---|---|
| Under 5 years | 26 |
| 5 years or more | 27 |
| 10 years or more | 28 |

The maximum annual leave entitlement is 28 days plus public holidays. Leave is administered in hours in Keel; one day equals 7.6 hours for a full-time employee. Part-time employees receive a pro rata entitlement.

## 4. Public holidays

Meridian NL observes the Dutch national public holidays, including King's Day and Liberation Day every year (Liberation Day is a paid day off at Meridian every year, not only in lustrum years). For the 2025 leave year 7 public holidays fall on working days:

| Holiday | Date |
|---|---|
| New Year's Day | Wednesday 1 January 2025 |
| Easter Monday | Monday 21 April 2025 |
| King's Day | Saturday 26 April 2025 (falls on a Saturday) |
| Liberation Day | Monday 5 May 2025 |
| Ascension Day | Thursday 29 May 2025 |
| Whit Monday | Monday 9 June 2025 |
| Christmas Day | Thursday 25 December 2025 |
| Second Christmas Day | Friday 26 December 2025 |

When a public holiday falls on a weekend, no replacement day is given. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a day off in lieu to be taken within three months, as well as any payment due under the Overtime, On-Call and Shift Allowance Policy (POL-HR-048).

### Holiday allowance

In addition to paid leave, NL employees receive a holiday allowance (vakantiegeld) of 8% of gross annual salary. The allowance accrues from June to May and is paid with the May payroll. Leavers receive accrued holiday allowance with their final pay. See the Payroll Calendar FY2025/26 - Netherlands for the payment date.

## 5. Booking leave

All annual leave is requested and approved in Keel via Keel > Time Off > Request Leave. Employees should give notice of at least twice the length of the leave requested, for example two weeks' notice for one week of leave. Managers should respond within five working days. Leave is not confirmed until it shows as approved in Keel, and travel should not be booked before approval.

Employees are entitled to take at least two consecutive weeks of leave during the summer period (May to September) if they wish. Managers may decline other requests where business needs require. Where more than two members of a team of eight or fewer request the same week, requests are normally approved in the order received.

## 6. Carry-over

NL leave is not capped at a fixed number of days but follows Dutch expiry rules. Statutory days not taken in the leave year expire on 1 July of the following year (six months after the end of the leave year). Extra-statutory days expire 5 years after the end of the leave year in which they were accrued. Keel always deducts leave from the days that expire first. Untaken leave is not paid in lieu except on leaving.

## 7. Buying additional leave

The NL flexible benefits menu does not include buying additional leave. Employees who need additional time off may request unpaid leave from their manager.

## 8. Joiners, leavers and part-time staff

Employees who join or leave during the leave year receive a pro rata entitlement for the months worked. On leaving, any accrued but untaken leave is paid in the final salary payment, and any leave taken in excess of accrued entitlement is deducted. Managers may ask leavers to use outstanding leave during their notice period.

## 9. Leave, sickness and family leave

Annual leave continues to accrue in full during sickness absence and during pregnancy, birth and partner leave. If an employee falls sick while on annual leave, they must report the sickness in line with the Sickness Absence Policy (POL-HR-017) and the affected days are reinstated. Family leave entitlements are set out in the Parental Leave Policy (POL-HR-031), which applies alongside Dutch statutory leave.
