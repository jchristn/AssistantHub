# Holiday and Leave Entitlement - United Kingdom

Policy code: POL-HR-014
Applies to: all employees employed by Meridian Instruments Ltd in the United Kingdom (Leeds headquarters and UK home-based staff)
Effective date: 1 April 2025
Owner: People team, Leeds (peoplehelp@meridian-instruments.example)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how much paid annual leave UK employees receive, which public holidays Meridian observes in the UK, and how leave is booked, carried over and paid. It is one of four regional leave policies. Colleagues in Rotterdam, Austin and Penang should refer to POL-HR-014-NL, POL-HR-014-US and POL-HR-014-MY respectively, which follow the same structure but contain different entitlements.

## 2. Leave year

The UK leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March, in line with the Meridian financial year. The 2025/26 leave year therefore runs from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026. Entitlement is refreshed in Keel on 1 April each year.

## 3. Annual leave entitlement

Full-time UK employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per leave year, in addition to public holidays. Entitlement increases with continuous service as shown below. Service increments are applied from the start of the leave year following the relevant service anniversary.

| Continuous service | Annual leave (days) |
|---|---|
| Under 3 years | 25 |
| 3 years or more | 26 |
| 5 years or more | 27 |

The maximum annual leave entitlement is 27 days plus public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro rata entitlement based on their contracted hours, calculated in hours in Keel.

## 4. Public holidays

Meridian observes the 8 bank holidays for England each year. For the 2025/26 leave year these are:

| Holiday | Date |
|---|---|
| Good Friday | Friday 18 April 2025 |
| Easter Monday | Monday 21 April 2025 |
| Early May bank holiday | Monday 5 May 2025 |
| Spring bank holiday | Monday 26 May 2025 |
| Summer bank holiday | Monday 25 August 2025 |
| Christmas Day | Thursday 25 December 2025 |
| Boxing Day | Friday 26 December 2025 |
| New Year's Day | Thursday 1 January 2026 |

Employees who are required to work on a bank holiday, for example in UK manufacturing (CC-3200), receive a day off in lieu to be taken within three months, as well as any payment due under the Overtime, On-Call and Shift Allowance Policy (POL-HR-048).

### Company closure days

Meridian UK closes on the three working days between Christmas and New Year. For 2025/26 the closure days are Monday 29, Tuesday 30 and Wednesday 31 December 2025. Employees must reserve 3 days of their annual leave entitlement to cover the closure. Staff who are required to work during the closure keep those 3 days to use at another time.

## 5. Booking leave

All annual leave is requested and approved in Keel via Keel > Time Off > Request Leave. Employees should give notice of at least twice the length of the leave requested, for example two weeks' notice for one week of leave. Managers should respond within five working days. Leave is not confirmed until it shows as approved in Keel, and travel should not be booked before approval.

Managers may decline requests where business needs require, for example during stock-take in manufacturing or at financial year end in Finance. Where more than two members of a team of eight or fewer request the same week, requests are normally approved in the order received.

## 6. Carry-over

UK employees may carry over up to 5 days of unused annual leave into the next leave year. Carried-over days must be used by 30 June of the new leave year; any carried-over days not used by 30 June are lost. Leave above the 5-day limit is lost at 31 March unless the employee was prevented from taking it by sickness or family leave, in which case section 9 applies. Untaken leave is not paid in lieu except on leaving.

## 7. Buying additional leave

UK employees may buy up to 5 additional days of annual leave through the flexible benefits scheme. Purchases are made during the annual enrolment window and the cost is spread across the year's payroll. See the Flexible Benefits Enrolment FAQ 2025 (UK) for the window dates and other options.

## 8. Joiners, leavers and part-time staff

Employees who join or leave during the leave year receive a pro rata entitlement for the months worked. On leaving, any accrued but untaken leave is paid in the final salary payment, and any leave taken in excess of accrued entitlement is deducted. Managers may ask leavers to use outstanding leave during their notice period.

## 9. Leave, sickness and family leave

Annual leave continues to accrue during sickness absence and during maternity, adoption, partner and shared parental leave. Where an employee is unable to take leave because of sickness or family leave, they may carry the untaken leave into the next leave year and must use it within 18 months of the end of the leave year in which it accrued. If an employee falls sick while on annual leave, they must report the sickness in line with the Sickness Absence Policy (POL-HR-017) and the affected days are reinstated. Family leave entitlements are set out in the Parental Leave Policy (POL-HR-031).

## 10. Contacts

Questions about UK leave entitlement should go to peoplehelp@meridian-instruments.example. Leave balances can be checked at any time in Keel under Time Off.
