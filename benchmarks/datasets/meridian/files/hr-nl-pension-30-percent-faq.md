# Netherlands Pension and 30% Ruling FAQ

Applies to: employees of Meridian Instruments B.V. in Rotterdam
Last updated: 15 January 2025
Contacts: benefits@meridian-instruments.example (pension), payroll-nl@meridian-instruments.example (30% ruling)

## Part 1: Pension

### Which pension scheme does Meridian NL use?

Meridian NL employees are members of the Zeevaart Pensioen scheme. It is a defined contribution (DC) scheme: contributions are paid into your personal pension account and invested, and the amount you receive at retirement depends on the contributions made and investment returns.

### When do I join?

You join automatically on your first working day. There is no waiting period and you cannot opt out, because participation is a condition of employment at Meridian NL.

### How much is paid in?

Meridian pays 8% of your pensionable salary and you pay 4%. Your contribution is deducted from your gross salary each month, so it reduces your taxable income.

### What is my pensionable salary?

Pensionable salary is your annual base salary plus the 8% holiday allowance, minus the franchise. The franchise is the part of your salary on which no pension is built up, because you will receive the Dutch state pension (AOW) on that part. For 2025 the franchise is €17,545. For example, if your base salary plus holiday allowance is €60,000, your pensionable salary is €42,455, Meridian pays €3,396.40 a year and you pay €1,698.20 a year.

### Is there a salary cap?

Yes. Pension is not built up on salary above the Dutch statutory maximum for pension accrual. Employees earning above the cap receive a gross net-pension allowance instead; ask benefits@meridian-instruments.example for details.

### What is included besides retirement savings?

The Zeevaart Pensioen scheme also includes a partner's pension and orphan's pension if you die while employed, and a disability pension (WIA supplement) if you become long-term disabled. These risk premiums are paid by Meridian on top of the 8%.

### Can I change how my pension is invested?

Yes. By default your account follows a lifecycle investment mix that becomes more defensive as you approach retirement. You can choose a more defensive or more offensive lifecycle on the Zeevaart Pensioen member portal.

### What happens when I leave Meridian?

Your pension account stays with Zeevaart Pensioen. You can leave it there or transfer it to your new employer's scheme.

## Part 2: The 30% ruling

### What is the 30% ruling?

The 30% ruling is a Dutch tax facility for employees recruited from abroad who have specific expertise that is scarce in the Dutch labour market. If granted, Meridian can pay up to 30% of your salary as a tax-free allowance to cover the extra costs of living and working in the Netherlands.

### Who qualifies?

You must have been recruited from outside the Netherlands, have lived more than 150 km from the Dutch border for at least 16 of the 24 months before starting, and meet the statutory salary norm. For 2025 the salary norm is €46,660 taxable salary per year (after the 30% allowance is deducted). For employees under 30 with a qualifying master's degree, the reduced norm for 2025 is €35,468.

### How do I apply?

Meridian and the employee apply jointly to the Belastingdienst (Dutch Tax Administration). Send your signed application form and a copy of your passport to payroll-nl@meridian-instruments.example. The application must be submitted within 4 months of your start date for the ruling to apply from your first day. If it is submitted later, it applies only from the first day of the month after the application.

### How long does it last?

The ruling lasts for a maximum of 5 years, reduced by any previous periods of stay or work in the Netherlands. Under current legislation the maximum tax-free percentage is reduced to 27% from 2027.

### Does the 30% ruling affect my pension?

Yes. Pensionable salary is based on your salary after the tax-free allowance, so your pension build-up is lower while the ruling applies. You can ask payroll-nl@meridian-instruments.example for a comparison.

### What if my salary falls below the norm?

If your taxable salary falls below the salary norm, for example after reducing hours, the ruling ends from that month. Tell payroll-nl@meridian-instruments.example about any change of hours as early as possible.
