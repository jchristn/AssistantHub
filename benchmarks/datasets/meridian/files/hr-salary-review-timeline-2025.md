# Salary Review Timeline FY2025/26 by Grade Band

Owner: Reward team (Head of Reward: Deepa Nair)
Issued: 14 April 2025
Audience: people managers at all sites
Contact: peoplehelp@meridian-instruments.example

## 1. Overview

The 2025 annual salary review covers all four sites (Leeds, Rotterdam, Austin and Penang). Review budgets by country are announced by the CEO on 28 April 2025. All increases take effect on 1 July 2025 and are paid for the first time in the July 2025 payroll at each site. Grade bands are defined in the Grade Structure, Salary Ranges and Bonus Targets 2025 document.

Manager proposals are entered in the Keel compensation module, which opens on Tuesday 6 May 2025. Each grade band has its own deadlines because calibration is run separately for each band.

## 3. Who calibrates

- G1–G3 and G4–G5: site calibration panels chaired by the site People Partner, with each department head.
- G6–G7: department heads with the Head of Reward.
- G8–G9: the Executive Committee (ExCo), chaired by the CEO.
- G10: the Remuneration Committee of the Board. No manager proposal is needed.

## 4. Eligibility

- Employees must have joined Meridian before 1 January 2025 to be eligible for a review increase.
- Employees on a performance improvement plan on the proposal deadline are not eligible; their pay is reviewed when the plan is completed.
- Employees on parental leave or long-term sickness absence are included in the review in the normal way.
- Employees promoted since 1 January 2025 are reviewed only if their promotion increase was less than 5%.

## 5. Manager guidance

1. Check each employee's position in range in Keel before proposing an increase.
2. Keep the team total within the country budget percentage; calibration panels may redistribute budget across teams.
3. Increases that take an employee above the maximum of their grade range are paid as a one-off lump sum instead of a base salary increase.
4. Do not discuss proposed increases with employees before letters are issued.
5. Letters are issued to employees in Keel on the dates above; managers should hold a conversation within five working days of the letter date.
