# Sickness Absence Policy

Policy code: POL-HR-017 | Owner: People Team | Effective date: 1 March 2025 | Version: 2

This version supersedes version 1 dated 1 November 2022.

## Summary of changes

- Sickness is now reported within 1 hour of your normal start time, to your manager and in Keel.
- Company sick pay at full pay extended for all service bands, followed by an equal period at half pay.
- The Bradford factor has been removed. Absence reviews are now triggered by 4 occasions or 10 working days of absence in a rolling 12 months.

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains what to do if you are too unwell to work, what pay you will receive, and how Meridian Instruments supports employees back to work after illness. It aims to treat people fairly and consistently while managing the impact of absence on colleagues and customers.

## 2. Scope

The policy applies to all employees at Leeds, Rotterdam, Austin and Penang. Where local law sets different requirements, the country notes in section 9 apply.

## 3. Reporting sickness

If you are unable to come to work because of illness or injury, you must contact your line manager within 1 hour of your normal start time on the first day of absence. A phone call is preferred; a message through Teams is acceptable if your manager does not answer. You must also record the absence yourself in Keel > Time Off > Report Sickness on the same day. If you are too unwell to do so, your manager will record it for you.

When you contact your manager, give the reason for your absence, how long you expect to be off, and any urgent work that needs to be covered. Keep your manager updated on each day of absence unless you have agreed a different contact pattern.

## 4. Evidence of sickness

For absences of up to 7 calendar days, you complete a Sickness Self-Certification form (F-330) on your return. The form is completed in Keel when you close the sickness record. For absences longer than 7 calendar days, you need a medical certificate (a fit note in the UK) from a doctor covering the whole period after day 7, uploaded to Keel.

Meridian may ask for a medical certificate for shorter absences where there is a pattern of frequent absence. Where Meridian requests this, the cost of the certificate is reimbursed.

## 5. Company sick pay

In addition to any statutory sick pay, Meridian pays company sick pay based on length of continuous service at the first day of absence. Entitlement is calculated over a rolling 12-month period. After the full-pay period, company sick pay continues at half pay for a period of the same length.

| Length of service | Full pay | Then half pay |
|---|---|---|
| Under 1 year | 6 weeks | 6 weeks |
| 1 to 3 years | 10 weeks | 10 weeks |
| Over 3 years | 16 weeks | 16 weeks |

When company sick pay is exhausted, only statutory sick pay (where applicable) is paid. Statutory sick pay is included within company sick pay, not paid in addition.

## 6. Monitoring absence

An absence review meeting is triggered when an employee has 4 separate occasions of absence, or 10 working days of absence in total, in a rolling 12-month period. The meeting is held by the line manager with a member of the People Team.

At the review, the manager will discuss the reasons for absence, any support needed, and whether a referral to occupational health would help. The review is supportive in the first instance. Absence related to a disability, pregnancy or an agreed long-term condition is discussed separately and adjustments are considered. Continued high absence may lead to action under the Disciplinary and Grievance Procedure (POL-HR-040).

## 7. Return to work

After every absence, the line manager holds a short return-to-work conversation on the employee's first day back. The conversation confirms that the employee is fit to return, checks whether any adjustments are needed, and is logged in Keel.

## 8. Long-term absence

Absence lasting more than 4 weeks is treated as long-term. The manager and People Team will keep in regular contact, arrange an occupational health referral with the employee's consent, and discuss a phased return, adjusted duties or other reasonable adjustments. The Employee Assistance Programme is available throughout.

## 9. Country notes

- UK: statutory sick pay is offset against company sick pay.
- Netherlands: Dutch law requires employers to pay at least 70% of salary for up to 104 weeks of illness. Meridian tops this up to 100% in the first year of illness. Sickness is also reported to the company doctor (bedrijfsarts) in line with the Gatekeeper Improvement Act, and the Dutch statutory reintegration process takes precedence over section 8.
- United States: absence for a serious health condition may also be protected under FMLA. Speak to the People Team.
- Malaysia: paid sick leave and hospitalisation leave are set out in the Holiday and Leave Entitlement - Malaysia policy (POL-HR-014-MY), which applies in place of section 5.

## 10. Sickness during annual leave

If you fall ill while on annual leave, you may treat the affected days as sickness if you report it as in section 3 and provide medical evidence for the whole period. The annual leave days are then reinstated.
