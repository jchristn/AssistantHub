# Halcyon HX-200 Quick-Start Guide

Document ID: prod-hx200-quick-start | Version: 1 | Effective: 2025-01-20 | Owner: Halcyon Product Engineering

This guide gets a new Halcyon HX-200 single-gas H2S detector into service. It does not replace the full user manual (prod-hx200-user-manual), which must be read before use.

## Before first use

1. Unpack the HX-200-KIT-01 and check that the detector, charger, calibration cap and certificate are present.
2. Charge the HX-BAT-200 battery fully in a safe area. Charging takes about 4 hours; a full charge gives about 36 hours of runtime.
3. Check the certification label: II 2G Ex ib IIB T4 Gb. The HX-200 is approved for Zone 1 and Zone 2, gas groups IIA and IIB only.

## Switching on

1. Press and hold ON/OK for 3 seconds.
2. Confirm that the LEDs flash, the sounder beeps and the unit vibrates.
3. Check the firmware version shown on screen (3.2.4) and the days to calibration.
4. Wait for the 30-second warm-up to finish.

## Daily bump test

Apply MI-CAL-H2S25 (25 ppm H2S) at 0.5 L/min through the calibration cap using regulator MI-REG-05. The reading must reach at least 20 ppm within 45 seconds and the alarm must sound. If the test fails, calibrate the instrument.

## Alarm set points

- Low alarm: 5 ppm
- High alarm: 10 ppm
- STEL: 15 ppm
- TWA: 5 ppm

If the high alarm sounds, leave the area immediately. Acknowledge the latched alarm with ON/OK only once you are in clean air.

## Calibration

Calibrate at least every 180 days. The yellow LED and "CAL DUE" indicate that calibration is overdue.

## Switching off

Press and hold ON/OK for 5 seconds until the countdown finishes.

## Need help?

Error codes (E-1nn) are explained in the Halcyon Error Code Reference. Raise support tickets at support.meridian-instruments.com.
