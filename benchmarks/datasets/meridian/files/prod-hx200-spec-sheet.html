Halcyon HX-200 Specification Sheet

Document ID: prod-hx200-spec-sheet | Effective: 2025-01-20 | Owner: Halcyon Product Marketing

The Halcyon HX-200 is a rugged, lightweight single-gas personal detector for hydrogen sulphide (H2S). It is designed for water treatment, sewer maintenance, pulp and paper, and oil and gas operators who need all-shift H2S protection with minimal maintenance.

Key features

  • 36-hour runtime from a single charge
  • 180-day calibration interval
  • 0.1 ppm H2S resolution
  • Only 210 g, IP66 rated
  • Shares menus and accessories with the HX-210 and HX-220
