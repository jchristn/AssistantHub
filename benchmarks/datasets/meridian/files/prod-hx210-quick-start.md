# Halcyon HX-210 Quick-Start Guide

Document ID: prod-hx210-quick-start | Version: 1 | Effective: 2025-06-16 | Owner: Halcyon Product Engineering

This guide gets a new Halcyon HX-210 four-gas detector into service. It does not replace the full user manual (prod-hx210-user-manual), which must be read before use.

## Before first use

1. Unpack the HX-210-KIT-03 and check that the detector, charger, calibration cap and certificate are present.
2. Charge the HX-BAT-210 battery fully in a safe area. Charging takes about 5 hours; a full charge gives about 14 hours of runtime.
3. Check the certification label: II 1G Ex ia IIC T4 Ga. The HX-210 is approved for Zone 0, Zone 1 and Zone 2, all gas groups.

## Switching on

1. Press and hold ON/OK for 3 seconds.
2. Confirm that the LEDs flash, the sounder beeps and the unit vibrates.
3. Check the firmware version shown on screen (3.4.1) and the days to calibration.
4. Wait for the 45-second warm-up to finish.

## Daily bump test

Apply MI-CAL-Q4-B at 1.0 L/min through the calibration cap using regulator MI-REG-10, or use the HX-DOCK-4. Each sensor must reach at least 80% of the cylinder value within 45 seconds and the alarms must sound. If the test fails, calibrate the instrument.

## Alarm set points

- H2S: low 5 ppm, high 10 ppm
- CO: low 30 ppm, high 100 ppm
- LEL: low 10% LEL, high 20% LEL
- O2: low 19.5% vol, high 23.0% vol

If the high alarm sounds, leave the area immediately. Acknowledge the latched alarm with ON/OK only once you are in clean air.

## Calibration

Calibrate at least every 120 days, following HX-210 Calibration Procedure version 2. The yellow LED and "CAL DUE" indicate that calibration is overdue.

## Switching off

Press and hold ON/OK for 5 seconds until the countdown finishes.

## Need help?

Error codes (E-1nn) are explained in the Halcyon Error Code Reference. Raise support tickets at support.meridian-instruments.com.
