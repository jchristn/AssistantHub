Halcyon HX-210 Specification Sheet

Document ID: prod-hx210-spec-sheet | Effective: 2025-06-16 | Owner: Halcyon Product Marketing

The Halcyon HX-210 is a rugged four-gas personal detector for oxygen, flammable gas, carbon monoxide and hydrogen sulphide. It is designed for confined space entry, refineries, chemical plants and utilities that need Zone 0 approval in a compact instrument.

Key features

  • Zone 0 approved: II 1G Ex ia IIC T4 Ga
  • 14-hour runtime from a single charge
  • 120-day calibration interval
  • Wide 0-200 ppm H2S range
  • 330 g, IP67 rated
