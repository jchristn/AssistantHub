# Halcyon HX-220 Quick-Start Guide

Document ID: prod-hx220-quick-start | Version: 1 | Effective: 2025-09-01 | Owner: Halcyon Product Engineering

This guide gets a new Halcyon HX-220 connected four-gas detector into service. It does not replace the full user manual (prod-hx220-user-manual), which must be read before use.

## Before first use

1. Unpack the HX-220-KIT-05 and check that the detector, charger, calibration cap, hard case, app licence card and certificate are present.
2. Charge the HX-BAT-220L battery fully in a safe area. Charging takes about 6.5 hours; a full charge gives about 22 hours of runtime.
3. Check the certification label: II 2G Ex ib IIC T4 Gb. The HX-220 is approved for Zone 1 and Zone 2 only. Do not take it into Zone 0.

## Switching on

1. Press and hold ON/OK for 3 seconds.
2. Confirm that the LEDs flash, the sounder beeps and the unit vibrates.
3. Check the firmware version shown on screen (3.4.2) and the days to calibration.
4. Wait for the 45-second warm-up and for the GPS icon to stop flashing.
5. Pair the Meridian Link Mobile app using Menu > Wireless > Pair.

## Daily bump test

Apply MI-CAL-Q4-B at 1.0 L/min through the calibration cap using regulator MI-REG-10, or use the HX-DOCK-4. Each sensor must reach at least 80% of the cylinder value within 45 seconds and the alarms must sound. If a pump HX-PMP-22 is fitted, also block the inlet to confirm the E-113 pump alarm.

## Alarm set points

- H2S: low 5 ppm, high 15 ppm
- CO: low 35 ppm, high 200 ppm
- LEL: low 10% LEL, high 25% LEL
- O2: low 19.5% vol, high 23.5% vol

The man-down pre-alarm starts after 60 seconds without motion. Move or press ON/OK within 15 seconds to cancel it.

## Calibration

Calibrate at least every 150 days. The yellow LED and "CAL DUE" indicate that calibration is overdue.

## Switching off

Press and hold ON/OK for 5 seconds until the countdown finishes.

## Need help?

Error codes (E-1nn) are explained in the Halcyon Error Code Reference. Raise support tickets at support.meridian-instruments.com.
