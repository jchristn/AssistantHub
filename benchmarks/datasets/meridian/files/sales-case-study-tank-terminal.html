Customer Case Study: Tessera and Halcyon at a Rotterdam Tank Terminal

Document ID: sales-case-study-tank-terminal | Version: 1 | Published: 2025-08-12 | Owner: Marketing, Rotterdam

Customer

Oostkade Tank Storage BV runs a 1.2 million cubic metre bulk liquid terminal in the Port of Rotterdam area. It stores fuels, biofuel blends and chemical intermediates in 86 tanks and handles about 2,400 barge and 140 seagoing vessel calls a year. The terminal employs 210 people, including 38 operators on a continuous five-shift rota.

Challenge

In 2023 Oostkade had two unplanned shutdowns of its main transfer pump house. One followed a bearing failure on a 250 kW product pump and cost about EUR 410,000 in demurrage and lost throughput. Vibration data came from a monthly handheld route, which missed the fault developing between rounds.

At the same time, the terminal's gas detection fleet of 120 older single-gas detectors from several suppliers was approaching end of life. Calibration records were kept on paper, and an external audit found 14% of instruments overdue for calibration.

Solution

Meridian's Rotterdam team proposed a combined deployment, installed in two phases between September 2024 and February 2025:

  • Tessera vibration monitoring on 52 critical assets: 118 TS-4e edge nodes and 3 TS-4 gateways (TS4-GW-ETH on the terminal fibre network), with a TesseraCloud Enterprise subscription for 36-month data retention. The jetty pump areas are classified Zone 2, so those nodes are the TS4E-NODE-3AX-Z2 variant running node firmware 2.8.0, with gateways on firmware 5.3.0.
  • Halcyon gas detection: 60 HX-210 four-gas instruments for tank and vessel entry teams, because Zone 0 certification was required for confined-space entry, and 45 HX-220 instruments for jetty patrols and lone workers. The HX-220 is limited to Zone 1 areas.
  • Four HX-DOCK-4 docking stations at the operator muster points, with automatic daily bump testing.
  • A Premier support contract for the site, with a named Technical Account Manager based in Rotterdam.

Oostkade estimates that the Tessera deployment paid for itself in about 11 months, based on the avoided demurrage from one detected pump bearing fault.

Customer view

"The dock changed our calibration culture overnight. Nobody argues with a green light," said the terminal's HSE manager. The maintenance lead added that TesseraCloud alarm routing sends pump alerts straight to the on-shift control room. That replaced a weekly spreadsheet review.

Lessons for similar deals

  • Confirm the hazardous area classification of every patrol route early. Mixed HX-210/HX-220 fleets are common at terminals.
  • Zone 2 Tessera nodes need gateway firmware 5.3.0. Check the customer's IT change window for gateway updates.
  • Terminal customers value 36-month data retention for insurer reporting. Lead with TesseraCloud Enterprise.
