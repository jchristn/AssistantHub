TesseraCloud Environment Configuration: Production (prod)

Document ID: eng-tesseracloud-config-prod. Owner: Platform Engineering. Last updated: 14 July 2025. This page describes the production environment only. Development and staging use different hosts, ports and settings; see their own configuration pages.

Environment summary

  • Environment name (TC_ENV): prod
  • Regions: euw1 (Dublin) primary; euc1 (Frankfurt) disaster recovery. See the TesseraCloud Regional Failover Runbook.
  • Forge environment: tesseracloud-prod. Deployments need manual approval by two maintainers and must run in a TesseraCloud change window (Tuesday and Thursday 06:00 to 08:00 UK time) unless they are zero-downtime deployments.
  • Data: customer telemetry, classified Confidential.
  • Access: operator roles only through just-in-time elevation (pamctl elevate --role tc-operator), maximum 12 hours.

Deployment

Production deployments of tc-agg and tc-stream use a canary stage (10% of consumers for 15 minutes), introduced after INC-2024-031. During a regional failover, a Forge deploy freeze is applied to tesseracloud-prod.
