Network and VPN Setup Guide: Austin

Document ID: it-network-vpn-guide-austin. Site: Austin, Texas, United States (Americas sales and Tessera software engineering, site code AUS). Owner: IT Operations, Network team. Last updated: 7 April 2025.

Who this guide is for

New starters and visitors working at the Austin office, and Austin-based staff connecting from home. Other sites have their own guide; subnets, VPN endpoints and printer queues are different at each site.

Wi-Fi

  • Meridian-Corp: company laptops only, 802.1X certificate authentication.
  • Meridian-Guest: visitors, sponsor approval by email, sessions last 12 hours.
  • Meridian-Demo: customer demo room only, for TS-4 gateway and TS-4e edge node demonstrations; internet access only.

DNS servers: 10.30.1.10 and 10.30.1.11. Internal domain: meridian.internal. Austin has no local servers; all services are reached over the site-to-site link to Leeds.

VPN (Meridian Connect)

  • Client: Meridian Connect, installed on every company laptop.
  • Austin endpoint: vpn-aus.meridian-instruments.com
  • Protocol and port: UDP 51820.
  • Tunnel mode: split tunnel. Traffic to 10.0.0.0/8 and meridian.internal goes through the VPN.
  • Authentication: directory account plus MFA (authenticator push with number matching, or FIDO2 key).
  • Idle timeout: 10 hours (longer than the 8 hours used at the European sites, to cover the time-zone overlap with Leeds).

If vpn-aus is unavailable, use vpn-lds.meridian-instruments.com as the fallback. Expect higher latency to Atlas when connected via Leeds.

Getting help

Raise a Beacon ticket in category NET-VPN, NET-WIFI or PRN-QUEUE. Austin has a part-time on-site IT technician (Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 CT); the Leeds service desk covers other times.
