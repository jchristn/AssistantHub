# ESD Control Standard

Document ID: mfg-esd-control-standard. Owner: Quality Engineering. Effective date: 14 October 2024. Applies to: Penang factory, Leeds engineering lab, Rotterdam Repair Centre.

Electrostatic discharge (ESD) can damage electronic assemblies without visible signs. Every person handling unpackaged boards or components must follow this standard.

## ESD protected areas

An ESD protected area (EPA) is marked with yellow floor tape and signs at every entrance. EPAs at Meridian: all four Penang SMT lines, Penang final test and calibration, the Leeds engineering lab benches, and the Rotterdam Repair Centre benches.

## Personal grounding

- Wrist straps are mandatory when seated at a workstation. Heel straps or ESD footwear are mandatory when walking in an EPA.
- Test your wrist strap and footwear at the entrance tester at the start of every shift and after every break. The tester passes between 750 kOhm and 35 MOhm.
- A failed test means you may not enter the EPA until the strap or footwear is replaced. Record the failure in the tester log.
- ESD smocks must be worn fully buttoned in all Penang EPAs.

## Workstations and environment

- Worksurface resistance to ground: below 1 x 10^9 ohm, verified monthly.
- Floor resistance to ground: below 1 x 10^9 ohm, verified quarterly.
- Ionisers on SMT line AOI and rework stations are checked quarterly for balance within plus or minus 35 V.
- Relative humidity in Penang EPAs is kept between 40% and 60%.

## Packaging

Boards leaving an EPA must be in shielding bags or conductive totes. Pink antistatic bags are not accepted for boards.

## Training and audit

All staff working in an EPA complete ESD training before first entry and every 12 months after that. Quality Engineering audits each EPA quarterly. Audit findings are raised in Beacon category MFG-OT for equipment faults and tracked in the Atlas quality module for process findings.
