# How to Report a Suspicious Email

Document ID: sec-phishing-reporting-guide. Owner: IT Security. Last updated: 18 November 2024.

If an email looks suspicious, do not click links, open attachments or reply. Report it using the Report Phish button in the mail client toolbar. The button forwards the message with full headers to the security mailbox and creates a Beacon ticket in category SEC-PHISH automatically.

If you cannot use the button (for example on a shop-floor kiosk in Penang), forward the email as an attachment to phish@meridian-instruments.com.

If you already clicked a link or entered your password:

1. Change your password immediately from a company device.
2. Call the IT Service Desk on extension 4357 (HELP), or +44 113 496 0400 from outside the network. The line is staffed 24 hours a day for security incidents.
3. Tell the desk exactly what you clicked and when. The desk will raise a SEC-INCIDENT ticket and IT Security will revoke your active sessions.

You will not be disciplined for reporting a mistake. Fast reporting is what limits damage.

IT Security runs simulated phishing campaigns every quarter. Simulated emails reported with the button are marked as a pass. Staff who click a simulated link are assigned a 10-minute refresher module in Keel, to be completed within 14 days.

Common signs of phishing at Meridian: supplier bank detail change requests (always verify by phone with a known contact), fake Atlas invoice approval notices, fake Keel payslip links, and messages pretending to be from the CTO asking for gift cards or urgent payments.
