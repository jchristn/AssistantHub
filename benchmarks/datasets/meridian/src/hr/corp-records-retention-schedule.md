# Records Retention Schedule (HR and Finance)

Policy code: POL-CORP-008. Owner: Data Protection Officer, Legal and Compliance (CC-5200). Effective date: 1 November 2024. Version: 1.

## 1. Purpose

This schedule sets the minimum and maximum periods for which Meridian keeps HR, payroll, recruitment, finance and compliance records. It supports the Data Protection Policy (POL-CORP-006), which requires personal data to be kept no longer than necessary. Where a record is subject to a legal hold notified by General Counsel, it must be retained until the hold is lifted, regardless of this schedule.

## 2. How to use this schedule

Retention periods run from the trigger event shown in the table. At the end of the period, records must be securely deleted or destroyed. System owners configure automatic deletion rules in Keel and Atlas; paper records are placed in confidential waste. Questions go to dpo@meridian-instruments.example.

## 3. Employee and HR records

| Record | Retention period | Trigger | System |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personnel file | 6 years | after leaving | Keel |
| Employment contract and changes | 6 years | after leaving | Keel |
| Right-to-work copies | 2 years | after employment ends | Keel |
| Absence and fit notes | 6 years | after leaving | Keel |
| Disciplinary warnings (spent) | 6 years | after leaving | Keel |
| Parental leave records | 6 years | after leaving | Keel |
| Training records | 6 years | after leaving | Keel |
| Conflict declarations (F-150) | 6 years | after declaration | Keel |
| Gifts register entries (F-140) | 6 years | after entry | Keel |
| Accident records | 3 years | after incident | Atlas EHS |
| Speak Up case files | 5 years | after case closure | VeritasLine portal |
| CCTV footage | 30 days | after recording | Site systems |

## 4. Recruitment records

| Record | Retention period | Trigger | System |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unsuccessful candidates | 6 months | after decision | Keel Recruit |
| Unsuccessful, with consent | 12 months | after decision | Keel Recruit |
| Interview scoring sheets | 6 months | after decision | Keel Recruit |
| Successful candidate file | moves to personnel file | on hire | Keel |
| Background check results | 6 months | after hire decision | Keel |

## 5. Payroll records by country

| Country | Payroll records | Tax and social security | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| United Kingdom | 6 years | 6 years | end of tax year |
| Netherlands | 7 years | 7 years | end of calendar year |
| United States | 7 years | 7 years | end of calendar year |
| Malaysia | 7 years | 7 years | end of calendar year |

Pension scheme records are kept for the life of the scheme membership plus 6 years, because pension entitlements can be queried long after an employee leaves.

## 6. Finance records

| Record | Retention period | Trigger | System |
|---|---|---|---|
| Expense claims and receipts | 7 years | after claim paid | Atlas |
| Corporate card statements | 7 years | after statement date | Atlas |
| Purchase orders and invoices | 7 years | after payment | Atlas |
| Supplier onboarding (F-410) | 7 years | after supplier deactivated | Atlas |
| Single source justifications (F-412) | 7 years | after PO closed | Atlas |
| Contracts | 7 years | after expiry | Atlas Contracts |
| Capital expenditure requests (F-420) | 10 years | after asset disposal | Atlas |
| Statutory accounts | permanent | n/a | Finance archive |

## 7. Responsibilities

- System owners configure and test automated deletion annually.
- Line managers must not keep personal copies of HR records outside Keel.
- The DPO audits compliance with this schedule every year and reports results to the Audit Committee.

## 8. Review

This schedule is reviewed every two years, or sooner if a legal requirement changes. Proposed changes must be approved by the DPO and General Counsel.
