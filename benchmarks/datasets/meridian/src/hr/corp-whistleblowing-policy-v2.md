# Speak Up Policy

Policy code: POL-CORP-004. Owner: General Counsel, Legal and Compliance (CC-5200). Effective date: 1 September 2025. Version: 2. This version supersedes POL-CORP-004 version 1, the Whistleblowing Policy dated 1 June 2022.

## 1. Purpose

Meridian Instruments Ltd wants everyone who works with us to feel safe speaking up about wrongdoing. Version 2 of this policy renames the Whistleblowing Policy as the Speak Up Policy, introduces the Meridian Speak Up portal, adds local hotline numbers for each country, shortens our acknowledgement time and reflects the Dutch Whistleblowers Authority Act (Wet bescherming klokkenluiders), which implements the EU Whistleblower Directive for our Rotterdam site.

## 2. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to all employees, officers, contractors, agency workers, interns, consultants, job applicants and former employees at all Meridian sites: Leeds, Rotterdam, Austin and Penang. Suppliers and their workers may also use the Speak Up channels.

## 3. What to report

You should speak up about any suspected wrongdoing connected with Meridian, including:

- fraud, theft, bribery, corruption or money laundering;
- breaches of law or regulation, including export control and competition law;
- risks to health and safety, including unsafe handling of calibration gases or test equipment;
- environmental damage;
- falsification of product test, calibration or certification records;
- misuse of personal data;
- modern slavery or labour abuses in our operations or supply chain;
- breaches of the Code of Conduct (POL-CORP-001) or financial controls;
- retaliation against anyone who has spoken up;
- deliberate concealment of any of the above.

Personal grievances about your own employment should normally be raised under the Disciplinary and Grievance Procedure (POL-HR-040).

## 4. How to raise a concern

You can speak to your line manager or any member of the Legal and Compliance team. If you prefer not to, or if you believe they are involved, use any of the following channels:

- the Meridian Speak Up portal, operated independently by VeritasLine and available 24 hours a day in English, Dutch and Malay, which allows anonymous reporting and two-way anonymous messaging;
- email speakup@meridian-instruments.example, read only by the Speak Up Officer and the Head of Internal Audit;
- telephone the VeritasLine hotline for your country:

The Speak Up Officer is Hannah Brightwell, General Counsel. Concerns about any member of the executive team or about the Speak Up Officer can be sent through the portal directly to the Chair of the Audit Committee, who oversees this policy.

The previous Clearline Ethics hotline closed on 31 August 2025. The former ethics mailbox now forwards automatically to speakup@meridian-instruments.example.

## 5. Confidentiality and anonymity

Your identity will be known only to those who need it to handle your report, and will not be disclosed without your consent unless required by law. You may report anonymously through the portal or hotline; you will receive a case number and password so that you can follow the case and answer questions without revealing who you are.

## 6. What happens next

1. The Speak Up Officer will acknowledge your report within 5 working days of receipt.
2. An initial assessment will determine whether an investigation is needed and who should lead it. Nobody implicated in a concern will be involved in handling it.
3. You may be asked for further information. You may be accompanied at any meeting by a colleague or trade union representative.
4. You will receive feedback on the action taken or planned within 3 months of the acknowledgement.

Speak Up case files are retained for 5 years after the case is closed, in line with the Records Retention Schedule (POL-CORP-008), and are then securely deleted.

## 7. Protection and support

Meridian will not tolerate retaliation of any kind against anyone who speaks up in good faith, including dismissal, demotion, loss of bonus, exclusion or harassment. This protection also applies to people who assist a reporter or who are named as witnesses. Retaliation is a serious disciplinary offence. Knowingly making false reports is also a disciplinary matter. Confidential support is available through the Employee Assistance Programme.

## 8. External disclosures

We encourage you to speak up internally first, but you may report to a competent external authority. In the Netherlands you may contact the Huis voor Klokkenluiders (Dutch Whistleblowers Authority) for advice or to report. In the United Kingdom, prescribed persons such as the Health and Safety Executive or the Serious Fraud Office may be appropriate. Legal and Compliance can advise on the right body in the United States and Malaysia.

## 9. Responsibility and review

The Speak Up Officer reports anonymised Speak Up statistics and themes to the Audit Committee every quarter. This policy will be reviewed every two years, or sooner if the law changes.
