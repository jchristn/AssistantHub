# Cost Centre Directory

Owner: Financial Planning and Analysis (fpa@meridian-instruments.example)
Effective: 1 April 2025 (FY2025/26)
System: Atlas (ERP)

## 1. How cost centres work

Every cost at Meridian Instruments is charged to a cost centre in Atlas. Cost-centre codes have the format CC-NNNN. The first digit identifies the function: 1 for corporate support functions, 2 for product engineering, 3 for manufacturing and quality, 4 for sales and 5 for executive and legal. Each cost centre has one budget holder, who approves spend against it within the limits in the Delegation of Authority (POL-FIN-003).

Employees charge expenses, training and purchases to their home cost centre unless their budget holder agrees a different one. Your home cost centre is shown in Keel under My Profile and in Atlas when you create a claim or requisition.

## 3. Currency and reporting

Cost centres are budgeted in their local currency and consolidated in GBP at the Group budget rates for FY2025/26 (GBP 1 = EUR 1.17 = USD 1.27 = MYR 5.90). Approval limits in the Delegation of Authority are expressed in GBP and converted at these rates for EUR, USD and MYR cost centres.

## 4. Requesting changes

New cost centres, closures and changes of budget holder are requested by email to fpa@meridian-instruments.example and approved by the Group Financial Controller, Oliver Pratt. Changes take effect from the first day of the following month. Budget reports for each cost centre are available in Atlas > Finance > Budget Reports; see the Budget Holder Guide for monthly and quarterly deadlines.
