Payroll Calendar FY2025/26 - Malaysia

Meridian Instruments (Malaysia) Sdn. Bhd. - Penang payroll. Issued by Payroll, Finance (CC-1200). Issue date: 10 March 2025. Applies to 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026. Contact: payroll-my@meridian-instruments.example.

1. How Malaysian pay works

Penang employees are paid monthly by bank transfer (IBG) to the bank account held in Keel. The standard pay date is the 26th of each month. If the 26th falls on a Saturday, Sunday or a public holiday observed in Penang, pay is made on the previous working day. Overtime, shift allowance and on-call payments for a month are paid with the following month's salary, in line with the Employment Act requirement to pay overtime within 10 days after the end of the wage period.

Payslips are available in Keel > My Pay > Payslips two working days before each pay date.

2. Cut-off dates

Change cut-off: changes that affect pay (new joiners, leavers, bank details, salary changes, overtime and shift claims, EPF rate elections) must be fully approved by the 15th of the month to be included in that month's payroll. If the 15th is not a working day, the cut-off is the previous working day. Changes received after the cut-off are processed the following month.

Expense claims approved in Atlas by the same cut-off (the 15th) are reimbursed with that month's salary. Medical claims submitted through Keel > Claims > Medical follow the same cut-off.

4. Statutory contributions and deductions

EPF, SOCSO, EIS contributions and PCB (monthly tax deduction) for each pay month are remitted to the relevant authorities by the 15th of the following month, as shown in the table. Contribution rates, including the Meridian EPF top-up, are explained in the Malaysia Statutory Contributions and Medical Benefits Guide.

5. Other key payroll dates

  • MIP bonus for FY2024/25: paid with the June 2025 payroll (Thu 26 Jun 2025).
  • Salary review increases effective 1 July 2025: first paid in the July 2025 payroll (Fri 25 Jul 2025).
  • Form EA for calendar year 2025: available in Keel > My Pay > Tax Documents by 28 February 2026.

6. Bank details and corrections

Update bank details in Keel > My Profile > Bank Details before the change cut-off. If you believe you have been underpaid by more than RM 500, contact payroll-my@meridian-instruments.example; Payroll will make an off-cycle correction payment within five working days. Smaller corrections are made in the next scheduled payroll.
