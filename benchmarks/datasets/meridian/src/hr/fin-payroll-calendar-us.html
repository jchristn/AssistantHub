Payroll Calendar FY2025/26 - United States

Meridian Instruments Inc. - US payroll (Austin). Issued by Payroll, Finance (CC-1200). Issue date: March 10, 2025. Applies to April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2026. Contact: payroll-us@meridian-instruments.example.

1. How US pay works

US employees are paid biweekly, every other Friday, by direct deposit to the bank account held in Keel. There are 26 pay dates in FY2025/26. The first pay date of the fiscal year is Friday, April 4, 2025. Each paycheck covers a two-week pay period ending the Friday one week before the pay date. If a pay date falls on a company holiday, pay is made on the previous business day.

Pay stubs are available in Keel > My Pay > Payslips two business days before each pay date.

2. Cut-off dates

Change cut-off: changes that affect pay (new hires, terminations, bank details, salary changes, timesheets for non-exempt employees, overtime and on-call claims, 401(k) deferral changes) must be approved by 12:00 Central Time on the Monday of the pay week. If that Monday is a company holiday, the cut-off moves to 12:00 CT on the previous Friday. Changes received after the cut-off are processed in the next pay period.

Expense reimbursement is not paid through payroll in the US. Expense claims approved in Atlas are reimbursed by a separate ACH payment every Wednesday. Claims approved by 17:00 CT on Monday are included in that week's Wednesday run.

4. Other key payroll dates

  • MIP bonus for FY2024/25: paid with the pay date of Friday, June 27, 2025.
  • Salary review increases effective July 1, 2025: first reflected in pay date 9 (Friday, July 25, 2025), with a retro adjustment for the partial period.
  • Form W-2 for calendar year 2025: available in Keel > My Pay > Tax Documents by January 31, 2026.
  • 401(k) deferrals and company match are deposited with the plan recordkeeper within three business days of each pay date.

5. Bank details and corrections

Update direct deposit details in Keel > My Profile > Bank Details before the change cut-off. A change of bank account triggers a prenote, so the first paycheck after a change may be issued as a paper check. If you believe you have been underpaid by more than $150, contact payroll-us@meridian-instruments.example; Payroll will issue an off-cycle payment within five business days. Smaller corrections are made in the next scheduled paycheck.
