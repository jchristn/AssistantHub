# Employee Handbook (2025 Edition)

Document code: HBK-HR-001. Owner: People Team (Chief People Officer, Adaeze Okafor). Effective date: 1 April 2025. Edition: 2025 (replaces the 2023 edition). Applies to: all employees of Meridian Instruments Ltd and its subsidiaries in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands, the United States and Malaysia.

## 1. Welcome to Meridian

### 1.1 A note from our CEO

Welcome to Meridian Instruments. Whether you have joined us in Leeds, Rotterdam, Austin or Penang, you are now part of a team of around 1,400 people who design, build and support equipment that keeps industrial sites safe and running. Our gas detectors protect people in confined spaces, our vibration monitoring warns engineers before a bearing fails, and our flow meters help customers use less energy and water. The work matters, and so do the people who do it.

This handbook is meant to be practical. It will not answer every question, but it should tell you where to look and who to ask. Please read it during your first two weeks and keep coming back to it. If something is unclear or out of date, tell the People Team so that the next edition is better.

Priya Raman, Chief Executive Officer

### 1.2 How to use this handbook

The handbook summarises how we work and points to the formal policies that sit behind each topic. Where a formal policy exists, the policy takes precedence over this summary. Policies are published in Keel, our HR portal, under Keel > Documents > Policies, and each has a policy code such as POL-HR-022 or POL-FIN-007. Local employment law always applies, and where a country supplement or a regional policy gives you a more favourable term, that term applies to you.

This handbook does not form part of your contract of employment unless your contract says so. Meridian may update it from time to time, and the current version is always the one published in Keel.

### 1.3 Who to ask

For most employment questions, start with your line manager. For questions about pay, leave, benefits, policies or your employment record, contact the People Help service centre at peoplehelp@meridian-instruments.example. For IT problems, including trouble logging in to Keel, raise a ticket in Beacon, our IT service desk. A full contacts list is in section 12.

## 2. About Meridian Instruments

### 2.1 Our business

Meridian Instruments Ltd is headquartered in Leeds in the United Kingdom. We design and manufacture industrial sensing equipment and sell it to customers in energy, water, chemicals, mining, food processing and building services. Our fiscal year runs from 1 April to 31 March, so FY2025/26 began on 1 April 2025.

### 2.2 Product lines

We have three product families:

- Halcyon gas detectors. The HX-200, HX-210 and HX-220 are closely related portable and fixed detectors. The HX-210 is our most common personal clip-on monitor and is also used inside our own labs.
- Tessera vibration monitoring. The TS-4 gateway and TS-4e edge node collect vibration data from rotating machinery, and TesseraCloud is our software-as-a-service platform for analysis and alerting.
- Lumen optical flow meters. The LF-60 and the pressure-rated LF-60P measure flow in pipes without contact with the fluid.

### 2.4 Leadership

The Executive Committee (ExCo) is led by Priya Raman (CEO) and includes Tomasz Wierzbicki (CFO), Adaeze Okafor (Chief People Officer), Lars Hedegaard (CTO) and the heads of our commercial and operations functions. Mei-Ling Tan is Head of IT Security. Hannah Brightwell is General Counsel.

### 2.5 Our values

Our four values are Precision, Integrity, Care and Ownership. Precision means we measure twice and get the detail right. Integrity means we do what we say and tell the truth when it is inconvenient. Care means we look after our colleagues, our customers and the places we work. Ownership means we take responsibility for outcomes rather than waiting to be asked. The Code of Conduct (POL-CORP-001) explains how these values translate into expected behaviour.

## 3. Your Employment

### 3.1 Your contract

You will receive a written contract or offer letter setting out your job title, grade, salary, working hours, notice period and place of work. Your grade (G1 to G10) determines your salary range, your bonus target and some benefits. The grade structure is published separately.

### 3.2 Probation

Most new employees serve a probationary period. The length depends on your country and is set out in the Probation Policy (POL-HR-011). During probation your manager will hold regular review meetings with you, record them in Keel and confirm the outcome in writing.

### 3.3 Right to work and pre-employment checks

Before you start we must confirm that you have the right to work in the country where you are employed. Some roles require additional checks, for example credit or criminal record checks for certain finance and cloud operations roles. These are described in the Recruitment and Selection Manual (POL-HR-052).

### 3.4 Working hours

Standard full-time weekly hours differ by country to reflect local law and practice. Unless your contract states otherwise, standard weekly hours are:

| Country | Standard weekly hours |
|---|---|
| United Kingdom | 37.5 |
| Netherlands | 38 |
| United States | 40 |
| Malaysia | 44 |

Part-time employees work a proportion of these hours and receive pay and most benefits on a pro rata basis. Hours for shift-based roles in manufacturing are set out in the relevant rota. If you are in the UK and wish to work more than the average of 48 hours per week permitted under the Working Time Regulations, you may sign an opt-out, but you are never required to do so.

### 3.5 Changes to your details

It is your responsibility to keep your personal details up to date. You can change your home address, emergency contacts and bank details in Keel. Bank details are changed under Keel > My Profile > Bank Details, and changes must be made before the payroll cut-off for your country if they are to take effect that month.

## 4. Pay and Payroll

### 4.1 How you are paid

Employees in the UK, the Netherlands and Malaysia are paid monthly. Employees in the United States are paid every two weeks. Pay dates and cut-offs for each country are published in the payroll calendar for the fiscal year, which you can find in Keel and on the Finance intranet page. Payslips are available under Keel > My Pay > Payslips, and year-end tax documents are available under Keel > My Pay > Tax Documents.

### 4.2 Payroll contacts

Each country has its own payroll mailbox: payroll-uk@meridian-instruments.example, payroll-nl@meridian-instruments.example, payroll-us@meridian-instruments.example and payroll-my@meridian-instruments.example. Please use the correct mailbox and include your employee number, but never send bank details or copies of identity documents by email.

### 4.3 Salary reviews

Salaries are reviewed once a year. The review is calibrated by grade band and new salaries normally take effect from 1 July. Each year the People Team publishes a timeline and the ExCo announces the pay review budget for each country.

### 4.4 Bonus

Eligible employees participate in the Meridian Incentive Plan (MIP), an annual bonus based on company performance and individual contribution. Your target bonus is a percentage of base salary linked to your grade. The payout factor for each year is announced after the year-end results are known.

### 4.5 Expenses

If you incur costs on company business you can claim them back through Atlas, our ERP system, under Atlas > Expenses > New Claim. The rules on what can be claimed, per-diem rates, hotel limits and who must approve a claim are set out in the Travel and Expense Policy (POL-FIN-007). Employees who travel regularly may be issued a corporate card under the Corporate Card Policy (POL-FIN-009).

### 4.6 Overpayments

If you are paid more than you are entitled to, you must tell payroll as soon as you notice. We will agree a reasonable repayment plan with you, and in the UK we may deduct the overpayment from later pay in line with your contract.

## 5. Where and When You Work

### 5.1 Hybrid working

Most office-based roles at Leeds, Rotterdam and Austin follow a hybrid pattern that combines office and home working. The minimum office attendance, anchor days, the home office allowance and the rules for working temporarily from another country are set out in the Remote and Hybrid Working Policy (POL-HR-022). Always check the current version of that policy, as the requirements have changed over time. Requests to work from abroad are made through Keel > Requests > Work From Abroad using form F-318.

### 5.2 Core hours and shift patterns

We want people to be able to plan their days, and we also need to be able to reach each other. For office-based and hybrid staff at Leeds, Rotterdam and Austin, core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you may start and finish earlier or later by agreement with your manager, provided your weekly hours are met and your team is covered.

Penang manufacturing runs a three-shift pattern: 07:00 to 15:00, 15:00 to 23:00 and 23:00 to 07:00. Shift allocations are published at least two weeks in advance by the production planning team. Shift and night allowances are covered by the Overtime, On-Call and Shift Allowance Policy (POL-HR-048). Leeds manufacturing uses a two-shift pattern agreed locally.

### 5.3 Flexible working requests

Any employee can ask for a change to their hours, times or place of work. Requests are made using form F-315 through Keel > Requests > Flexible Working. Your manager will meet with you to discuss the request and give you a decision in writing. In the UK we handle requests in line with the statutory procedure, and in the Netherlands in line with the Flexible Working Act.

### 5.4 Overtime and on-call

Some grades are eligible for paid overtime, and some teams, such as TesseraCloud Operations, run an on-call rota. Overtime must be approved in advance. Rates and allowances are in POL-HR-048 and are claimed on form F-340.

### 5.5 Business travel

Travel should be booked through Atlas > Travel > Book Trip, which connects to our travel management company. International trips need pre-approval on form F-221. See the Travel Booking Guide and POL-FIN-007 for details.

## 6. Time Off

### 6.1 Annual leave

Your annual leave entitlement, leave year, public holidays and carry-over rules depend on the country you work in. There is a separate Holiday and Leave Entitlement policy for each country: POL-HR-014 (UK), POL-HR-014-NL, POL-HR-014-US and POL-HR-014-MY. Leave is requested and approved in Keel under Keel > Time Off > Request Leave. Please give your manager reasonable notice, particularly for longer periods, and check the team calendar before booking.

### 6.2 Sickness absence

If you are unwell and cannot work, tell your manager as early as possible and record the absence in Keel under Keel > Time Off > Report Sickness. The Sickness Absence Policy (POL-HR-017) explains reporting deadlines, self-certification on form F-330, when a medical certificate is needed, company sick pay and how we support you on your return.

### 6.3 Parental leave

We offer enhanced pay for birth parents, adopters and partners. The Parental Leave Policy (POL-HR-031) sets out eligibility, pay and notice requirements. Notify us using form F-301. The policy was substantially improved in 2025, so make sure you are reading the current version.

### 6.4 Other leave

Local law gives you further rights to time off, for example for dependants in an emergency or for antenatal appointments. The country leave policies describe these. If you are facing a difficult personal situation, speak to your manager or People Help; we will always try to be flexible.

### 6.5 Recording absence accurately

Accurate records protect you and help us plan. Managers must approve or decline leave requests in Keel within five working days, and must make sure that any sickness reported by phone is also entered in Keel.

## 8. Health, Safety and Wellbeing

### 8.1 Our commitment

Our products exist to keep people safe, and we hold ourselves to the same standard. Every employee has a duty to take reasonable care of their own health and safety and that of others, to follow safe systems of work, and to report hazards, near misses and accidents. No job is so urgent that it cannot be done safely.

### 8.2 Site inductions

Every new starter receives a site safety induction on their first day, covering fire evacuation routes, assembly points, first aid, and any site-specific hazards. Visitors must be signed in at reception and escorted at all times in production and laboratory areas. Contractors must complete the contractor induction and hold a valid permit to work where one is required.

### 8.3 Personal protective equipment

Where a risk assessment requires personal protective equipment (PPE), it is provided free of charge and you must wear it. Safety footwear and high-visibility clothing are required on all production floors in Leeds and Penang. Hearing protection is mandatory in the Penang machining hall and in the Leeds enclosure fabrication area. Damaged PPE must be replaced before work continues.

### 8.4 Laboratories and controlled areas

Several of our laboratories contain hazards that are not present in ordinary office areas, including calibration gases, pressurised cylinders, lasers and rotating test rigs. Access is controlled by badge and each area has a named area owner.

The Halcyon calibration lab in Leeds, Building C, holds certified test gases including hydrogen sulphide, carbon monoxide and methane mixtures used to calibrate and verify HX-200, HX-210 and HX-220 detectors. Anyone entering the Halcyon calibration lab must first complete the gas-safety induction GS-02, and must wear a bump-tested personal HX-210 monitor at all times while inside. Bump tests are carried out at the lab entrance station at the start of each visit and the result is logged automatically against your badge. If your monitor alarms, leave the lab immediately by the nearest exit, close the door behind you and alert the area owner. The GS-02 induction must be refreshed every two years.

The Lumen flow rig in Rotterdam and the Tessera vibration test cells in Austin have their own inductions, which are arranged by the area owners. Laser safety training is required before operating any Lumen test bench with the cover removed.

### 8.5 Display screen equipment and home working

If you use a computer for a significant part of your working day, you should complete the display screen equipment (DSE) self-assessment in Keel within your first month and whenever your workstation changes. Hybrid workers should also assess their home workstation. Equipment for home working can be requested from IT through Beacon.

### 8.6 First aid and fire safety

Each site has trained first aiders and fire marshals, whose names and photographs are displayed on noticeboards near the main entrances. If you would like to volunteer as a first aider or fire marshal, speak to your site facilities team; training is provided and paid for by Meridian. Fire alarms are tested weekly at a set time at each site, and full evacuation drills take place at least twice a year.

### 8.7 Reporting accidents and near misses

All accidents, injuries, dangerous occurrences and near misses must be reported within 24 hours using the incident reporting form on the Health and Safety intranet page, even if nobody was hurt. Your manager and the site health and safety adviser will review the report and decide whether an investigation is needed. Some incidents must be reported to the regulator in the relevant country, and the site health and safety adviser will handle this. Never alter the scene of a serious incident except to make it safe or to help an injured person.

### 8.8 Wellbeing

Your mental health matters as much as your physical health. Mental health first aiders are available at every site, and the EAP is available at any time. Managers are expected to hold regular one-to-ones and to notice when a team member seems to be struggling. If you are worried about a colleague, speak to them, to your manager or to People Help.

### 8.9 Alcohol and drugs

You must not work under the influence of alcohol or drugs that could impair your performance or safety. This is especially important in production, laboratory and driving roles. If you are taking prescribed medication that might affect your ability to work safely, tell your manager or occupational health. Alcohol may be served at approved company events, but moderation and good judgement are expected.

## 9. Conduct and Standards

### 9.1 The Code of Conduct

The Code of Conduct (POL-CORP-001) sets out the standards of behaviour we expect from everyone at Meridian, including rules on conflicts of interest, anti-bribery, gifts and hospitality, competition law and trade compliance. All employees must complete Code of Conduct training when they join and every year after that.

### 9.2 Dignity at work

We are committed to a workplace free from harassment, bullying, discrimination and victimisation. Everyone should be treated with courtesy and respect, regardless of age, disability, gender identity, marital status, pregnancy, race, religion or belief, sex or sexual orientation. Concerns can be raised with your manager, People Help, or through the Speak Up channels described in POL-CORP-004.

### 9.3 Dress and appearance

There is no formal dress code in office areas. Please dress in a way that is appropriate for the day, particularly when meeting customers or visitors. In production and laboratory areas, clothing must be compatible with the required PPE, and loose jewellery and long hair must be secured near moving machinery.

### 9.4 Personal relationships at work

We recognise that close personal relationships sometimes develop between colleagues. If you are in a close personal relationship with someone in your reporting line, or with someone whose work you approve, audit or pay, you must declare it using the Conflict of Interest Declaration (F-150) so that we can make appropriate arrangements. The Code of Conduct gives more detail on declarations.

### 9.5 Social media and public statements

You are welcome to share Meridian news on your personal social media accounts. Do not share confidential information, customer names without permission, or photographs taken in production or laboratory areas. Only authorised spokespeople may speak to the media on behalf of the company. Any media enquiry should be passed to the communications team.

### 9.6 Recognition and long-service awards

We like to say thank you. Managers can nominate colleagues for quarterly Values Awards, and each site holds a recognition event at the end of the fiscal year. We also mark long service with awards on the anniversary of your start date, as follows:

| Years of service | Award |
|---|---|
| 5 years | £250 voucher |
| 10 years | £1,000 plus 5 extra days of leave (one-off) |
| 20 years | £2,500 plus 10 extra days of leave (one-off) |
| 25 years | £4,000 |

Awards outside the UK are paid in the local currency equivalent at the Group budget exchange rate. The extra days of leave are a one-off addition to your entitlement and should be taken within 12 months of the anniversary. Cash awards are paid through payroll and are subject to tax and social security in the normal way.

### 9.7 Performance and development

Every employee agrees objectives with their manager at the start of the fiscal year, has a mid-year check-in in October, and a year-end review in April. Reviews are recorded in Keel. If performance falls short, your manager will first discuss it informally and agree support. Where problems continue, the Disciplinary and Grievance Procedure (POL-HR-040) may apply.

### 9.8 Raising a grievance

If you have a concern about your work, working conditions or relationships with colleagues, try to resolve it informally with your manager first. If that does not work, you can raise a formal grievance under POL-HR-040.

## 10. Security, Systems and Information

### 10.1 Our core systems

You will use several internal systems during your time at Meridian:

- Keel, the HR portal, for leave, sickness, payslips, benefits, requests and declarations.
- Atlas, the ERP, for expenses, purchasing, travel booking and finance reporting.
- Beacon, the IT service desk, for IT support, equipment and access requests.
- Forge, the CI/CD platform, used by engineering teams to build and release software.

Access is granted according to your role. Requests for additional access are raised in Beacon and approved by your manager and, for some systems, the system owner.

### 10.2 Information security

The IT Security team, led by Mei-Ling Tan, sets the rules for passwords, multi-factor authentication, acceptable use of IT and handling of classified information. These rules are in the IT policies published by IT. Report suspected phishing, lost devices or security incidents immediately through Beacon.

### 10.3 Badges and site access

Every employee is issued with a photo badge that opens the doors you need for your role and identifies you as a Meridian employee. Wear your badge visibly at all times on site, do not lend it to anyone, and do not allow people you do not recognise to follow you through secure doors.

If you lose your badge, report the loss to the site security desk at security@meridian-instruments.example within 24 hours so that it can be deactivated. The first replacement badge is issued free of charge. A second replacement within 12 months costs £15 (or the local currency equivalent), which is deducted from pay with your agreement. Temporary day passes can be collected from reception while a replacement is printed, but they do not open laboratory or production doors.

### 10.4 Company equipment

Laptops, phones and other equipment remain Meridian property. Look after them, keep them secure when travelling, and return them when you leave or when asked. Loss or theft must be reported through Beacon straight away, and theft must also be reported to the police.

### 10.5 Personal data

Meridian holds personal data about you to manage your employment. The Data Protection Policy (POL-CORP-006) explains how we use it, how long we keep it and how you can request a copy. If you handle other people's personal data in your role, you must follow the same policy.

### 10.6 Confidential information

During your employment you will have access to confidential information about our products, customers, suppliers and finances. You must not share it outside Meridian except where authorised, and your confidentiality obligations continue after you leave.

## 11. Leaving Meridian

### 11.1 Resigning

If you decide to leave, please tell your manager first and then confirm your resignation in writing, by email or through Keel. Your manager will confirm your last day of employment and agree a handover plan with you.

### 11.2 Notice periods

Unless your contract says otherwise, the following notice periods apply to UK employees, both from you to Meridian and from Meridian to you:

| Grade | Notice period |
|---|---|
| G1–G3 | 1 month |
| G4–G7 | 2 months |
| G8–G9 | 3 months |
| G10 | 6 months |

During probation, the notice period is 1 week on either side. Statutory notice may be longer for employees with long service, in which case the statutory period applies to notice given by Meridian. Notice periods in the Netherlands, the United States and Malaysia are set in local contracts and follow local law.

### 11.3 Garden leave and payment in lieu

Meridian may ask you not to attend work or contact customers during all or part of your notice period (garden leave). You remain an employee during garden leave and continue to receive pay and benefits. Where your contract allows, we may pay you in lieu of some or all of your notice.

### 11.4 Before you go

Your manager will complete the Leaver Checklist (F-110) with you. This includes returning equipment and badges, transferring knowledge and files, clearing any outstanding expenses or corporate card transactions, and confirming your final pay and any untaken leave. You will be invited to an exit interview, which is optional but helps us improve.

### 11.5 References

Meridian provides factual references confirming your dates of employment and job title. Requests should be sent to peoplehelp@meridian-instruments.example. Managers must not give personal references on behalf of the company.

### 11.6 After you leave

We keep your personnel records for a period after you leave in line with the Records Retention Schedule (POL-CORP-008). Your P45 (UK) or equivalent will be issued after your final pay, and you will keep access to Keel payslips for 90 days after your leaving date.
