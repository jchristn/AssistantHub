# Holiday and Leave Entitlement - United States

Policy code: POL-HR-014-US
Applies to: all employees employed by Meridian Instruments Inc. in the United States (Austin site and US remote staff)
Effective date: 1 April 2025
Owner: People team, Austin (peoplehelp@meridian-instruments.example)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how much paid time off (PTO) US employees receive, which holidays Meridian observes in the US, and how time off is requested, carried over and paid. It is one of four regional leave policies. Colleagues in Leeds, Rotterdam and Penang should refer to POL-HR-014, POL-HR-014-NL and POL-HR-014-MY respectively, which follow the same structure but contain different entitlements.

## 2. Leave year

The US leave year runs from April 1 to March 31, in line with the Meridian fiscal year. The 2025/26 leave year therefore runs from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2026. Balances roll over in Keel on April 1 each year.

## 3. Annual leave entitlement

Full-time US employees receive 20 days (160 hours) of PTO per leave year, in addition to company holidays. PTO is used for vacation and personal time; sick time is separate (see section 9). PTO accrues each biweekly pay period rather than being granted up front.

| Continuous service | PTO (days) | Accrual per biweekly period |
|---|---|---|
| Under 5 years | 20 | 6.15 hours |
| 5 years or more | 25 | 7.69 hours |

The maximum PTO entitlement is 25 days per year. Part-time employees accrue PTO pro rata to their scheduled hours. Employees may take PTO once accrued; with manager approval, up to 40 hours may be taken in advance of accrual.

### Floating holidays

Each employee receives 2 floating holidays per leave year to use on days of personal, cultural or religious significance. Floating holidays are requested like PTO, must be used within the leave year, cannot be carried over and are not paid out on leaving. New hires joining after October 1 receive 1 floating holiday for that leave year.

Non-exempt employees who work on a company holiday, for example in TesseraCloud Operations (CC-2210), are paid in line with the Overtime, On-Call and Shift Allowance Policy (POL-HR-048).

## 5. Booking leave

All PTO and floating holidays are requested and approved in Keel via Keel > Time Off > Request Leave. Employees should give notice of at least twice the length of the leave requested, for example two weeks' notice for one week of PTO. Managers should respond within five working days. Time off is not confirmed until it shows as approved in Keel, and travel should not be booked before approval.

Managers may decline requests where business needs require, for example around major TesseraCloud releases or at fiscal year end. Where more than two members of a team of eight or fewer request the same week, requests are normally approved in the order received.

## 6. Carry-over

US employees may carry over a maximum of 5 days (40 hours) of unused PTO into the next leave year. Any balance above 40 hours on March 31 is forfeited. Carried-over PTO does not expire during the following leave year but counts toward the 40-hour cap at the next year end.

## 7. Buying additional leave

The US benefits program does not include buying additional PTO. Employees who need additional time off may request unpaid personal leave from their manager.

## 8. Joiners, leavers and part-time staff

New hires begin accruing PTO from their first pay period. On leaving, accrued but unused vacation PTO is paid out in the final paycheck. Floating holidays and sick time are not paid out. Any PTO taken in advance of accrual and not yet earned is deducted from final pay where permitted by law.

## 9. Leave, sickness and family leave

US employees receive 7 days (56 hours) of paid sick time per leave year, separate from PTO. Sick time is credited on April 1 (pro rata for new hires), may be used for the employee's own illness or to care for a family member, is not carried over and is not paid out on leaving. Reporting requirements are set out in the Sickness Absence Policy (POL-HR-017). PTO continues to accrue during paid parental leave under the Parental Leave Policy (POL-HR-031). Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) leave runs concurrently with Meridian leave where the employee is eligible.
