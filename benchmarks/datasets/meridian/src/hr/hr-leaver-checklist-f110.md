Form number: F-110. Owner: People Operations. Version date: 1 February 2025. Start this form in Keel as soon as a resignation is accepted or a leaving date is agreed. Queries to peoplehelp@meridian-instruments.example.

## Section B: Line manager actions

1. Confirm the resignation in writing through Keel within 5 working days of receiving it.
2. Agree a knowledge transfer plan with the leaver covering open work, documents, customer and supplier contacts, and system ownership. Record the named person taking over each item.
3. Reassign any approval roles held by the leaver in Atlas (budget holder, requisition approver) and in Keel (people manager).
4. Agree how remaining annual leave will be handled: taken during notice or paid in final pay.
5. Raise a Beacon "Leaver Return" request at least 5 working days before the last working day so that IT can schedule collection of equipment and removal of access.
6. Confirm whether the leaver holds a corporate card and notify cards@meridian-instruments.example.

## Section C: Leaver actions

1. Hand over work and files according to the knowledge transfer plan. Do not delete shared documents or mailbox content.
2. Submit all outstanding expense claims in Atlas > Expenses > New Claim before the last working day.
3. Reconcile the final corporate card statement in Atlas > Cards > Reconcile Statement and return the card for cancellation.
4. Return laptop, phone, peripherals, building pass and any test equipment or product samples through the Beacon "Leaver Return" request.
5. Check that personal contact details in Keel are up to date so that final payslip and tax documents reach you.
6. Complete the exit interview.

## Section D: People Operations and Payroll

1. Calculate final pay, including salary to the last working day and payment for accrued but untaken annual leave, less any leave taken in excess of accrual.
2. Apply any agreed deductions, such as training fee repayment under F-265, where local law allows.
3. Issue the final payslip and leaver tax documents through Keel.
4. Send the exit interview invitation (online survey or conversation with a People Partner).
5. Close the Keel record after the final payroll run.

## Section E: IT and Finance

1. IT disables network, email and system access at 17:00 local time on the last working day unless otherwise agreed.
2. Finance cancels the corporate card with Northgate Commercial Card and closes any open advances.
3. Finance removes the leaver from Atlas approval workflows.
