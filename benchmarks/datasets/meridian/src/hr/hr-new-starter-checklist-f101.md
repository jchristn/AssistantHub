## Section B: Before the start date (hiring manager)

1. Confirm the start date, working pattern and site with Talent Acquisition once pre-employment checks are cleared.
2. Raise a Beacon request for a laptop, phone and standard software at least 10 working days before the start date, using the Beacon catalogue item "New Starter Equipment".
3. Request any additional system access (for example Atlas roles or Forge access) through Beacon, naming the approver for each system.
4. Confirm the cost centre with Finance so that the employee is assigned to the correct cost centre in Atlas. Codes are listed in the Cost Centre Directory.
5. Arrange a building pass with the site security desk (security@meridian-instruments.example).
6. Nominate an onboarding buddy from the team.
7. Prepare a first-week schedule and three to five probation objectives.

## Section C: Before the start date (People Operations)

1. Create the employee record in Keel from the accepted offer, including grade, salary, contract type and probation end date.
2. Send the welcome pack and Keel login instructions to the employee's personal email.
3. Set up payroll with the local payroll team and confirm the first pay date.
4. Enrol the employee in the default pension or retirement plan for their country.
5. Schedule the day-one induction.

## Section D: Day one

1. Collect building pass and equipment.
2. Attend the day-one security induction covering building access, badge rules, visitor escorting and reporting lost badges.
3. Attend the site health and safety briefing. Staff who will enter laboratory or production areas complete the site-specific safety induction before entry.
4. Log in to Keel and check personal details, emergency contacts and bank details at Keel > My Profile > Bank Details.
5. Meet the line manager and onboarding buddy.
