# Overtime, On-Call and Shift Allowance Policy

Policy code: POL-HR-048. Owner: Reward, People Team. Effective date: 1 August 2024. Applies to: all Meridian Instruments sites, subject to local law and contracts.

## 1. Purpose

Some of our work cannot be done within normal hours. Production lines in Penang run around the clock, TesseraCloud must be supported out of hours, and field and calibration work sometimes runs late. This policy explains who is paid for overtime, the rates that apply, how on-call cover is rewarded and how shift allowances work.

## 2. Overtime eligibility

Paid overtime applies to employees in grades G1 to G4 only. Employees in grade G5 and above are expected to manage their working time flexibly and are not paid for additional hours, although managers may agree time off in lieu for exceptional periods.

In the United States, employees classified as non-exempt under the Fair Labor Standards Act are always paid overtime for hours over 40 in a workweek, whatever their grade. In Malaysia, overtime for employees covered by the Employment Act 1955 follows the statutory minimum rates where these are higher than the rates below.

## 3. Pre-approval

Overtime must be approved in advance by the line manager. The request and the claim are both made on form F-340 Overtime and On-Call Claim. Unapproved overtime will not be paid except in a genuine emergency, where approval must be sought on the next working day.

## 4. Overtime rates

| When worked | Rate |
|---|---|
| Weekdays, beyond contracted hours | 1.5x basic hourly rate |
| Saturdays | 1.5x basic hourly rate |
| Sundays | 2x basic hourly rate |
| Public holidays | 2x basic hourly rate |

Overtime is paid for actual time worked in blocks of 15 minutes. Employees working on a public holiday also keep the right to a replacement day where their local leave policy provides for one.

## 5. On-call arrangements

On-call means being available to respond by phone or remotely outside normal hours and, if needed, to attend site. On-call rotas are agreed by the Head of Department and published at least four weeks ahead.

On-call is paid as a flat allowance for each period on the rota, whether or not the employee is called out. Time actually worked during a call-out is paid as overtime for eligible grades, or recorded as time off in lieu for G5 and above.

| Team or site | Weekday night | Weekend or public holiday day |
|---|---|---|
| UK (all teams) | £30 | £60 |
| TesseraCloud Operations, Austin (CC-2210) | $45 | $90 |
| Rotterdam | €35 | €70 |

A weekday night runs from the end of the working day until the start of the next working day. A weekend or public holiday day is a 24-hour period starting at 00:00.

In the Netherlands, on-call rotas must also comply with the rest period rules in the Working Hours Act, and managers must make sure employees receive the required uninterrupted rest after a call-out.

## 6. Shift allowance in Penang

Manufacturing (CC-3100) and Quality Assurance (CC-3110) teams in Penang work a three-shift pattern. Employees on the night shift receive a night-shift allowance of RM 15 per shift. Day and afternoon shifts do not attract an allowance. The shift allowance is paid in addition to any overtime for hours beyond the scheduled shift.

## 7. Limits and wellbeing

- No employee should work more than 12 hours in a single day, including overtime, except in an emergency.
- UK employees may opt out of the 48-hour average weekly limit only by signing a written opt-out, which can be withdrawn at any time.
- No employee should be on the on-call rota for more than one week in every three without their agreement.
- Managers must monitor overtime levels monthly and discuss sustained high overtime with their People Partner.

## 8. Claiming

Overtime, on-call and shift allowances are claimed on F-340 and approved by the line manager in Keel. Claims must be approved by the payroll change cut-off for the month (or for the pay period in the US) to be included in that pay run. Cut-off dates are listed in the payroll calendar for each country. Late claims are paid in the following pay run.
