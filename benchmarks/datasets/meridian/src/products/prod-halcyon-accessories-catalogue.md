# Halcyon Accessories and Spare Parts Catalogue

Document ID: prod-halcyon-accessories-catalogue | Edition: FY2025/26 | Effective: 2025-04-01 | Owner: Halcyon Product Management

This catalogue lists the accessories, consumables and spare parts for the Halcyon HX-200, HX-210 and HX-220 portable gas detectors. It does not show prices. For prices in GBP, EUR, USD and MYR see the Meridian Price List FY2025/26 (sales-price-list-fy25-26). Parts not listed there are available on request from the regional sales office.

## Sensors

| Part number | Description | Fits | Typical life |
|---|---|---|---|
| HX-SNS-H2S-02 | H2S electrochemical sensor | HX-200, HX-210, HX-220 | 36 months |
| HX-SNS-CO-03 | CO electrochemical sensor | HX-210, HX-220 | 36 months |
| HX-SNS-LEL-04 | Catalytic bead LEL sensor | HX-210, HX-220 | 36 months |
| HX-SNS-O2-01 | O2 electrochemical sensor | HX-210, HX-220 | 24 to 30 months |

Sensors are the same part across models, but the instrument firmware applies model-specific ranges. For example, the same HX-SNS-H2S-02 reads 0-100 ppm in an HX-200 or HX-220 and 0-200 ppm in an HX-210.
