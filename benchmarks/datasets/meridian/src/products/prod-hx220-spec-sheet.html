Halcyon HX-220 Specification Sheet

Document ID: prod-hx220-spec-sheet | Effective: 2025-09-01 | Owner: Halcyon Product Marketing

The Halcyon HX-220 is a connected four-gas personal detector for oxygen, flammable gas, carbon monoxide and hydrogen sulphide, with Bluetooth, GPS and man-down protection for lone workers. It is designed for remote, cold-climate and high-noise sites.

Key features

  • Bluetooth LE 5.0, GPS and man-down alarm
  • 22-hour runtime from a single charge
  • 150-day calibration interval
  • Operates down to -40 C
  • 103 dB alarm for noisy environments
  • Optional HX-PMP-22 sampling pump
