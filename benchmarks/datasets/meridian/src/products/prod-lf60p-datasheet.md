# Lumen LF-60P Optical Flow Meter Datasheet

Document ID: prod-lf60p-datasheet | Version: 1 | Effective: 2025-02-03 | Owner: Lumen Product Management, Rotterdam

## Overview

The Lumen LF-60P is an inline optical-correlation flow meter for clean and lightly loaded liquids in process industry, high-pressure water injection and hot water applications. A pulsed LED light sheet passes through two sapphire windows in the measuring tube, and the electronics cross-correlate the particle and micro-bubble patterns seen by two receivers to calculate flow velocity. With no moving parts and no obstruction in the bore, the LF-60P produces negligible pressure loss and needs no routine recalibration.

The LF-60P is the high-pressure member of the Lumen family. It is rated PN40 (40 bar) and suits pipe sizes from DN25 to DN150. For low-pressure utility work, or for DN200 lines, the LF-60 is usually the more economical choice.

## Key features

- Optical correlation measurement with a ±0.75% of reading accuracy across 0.5 to 15 m/s
- Full bore design with no moving parts and no pressure drop worth measuring
- 4-20 mA with HART 7, scaled pulse and Modbus RTU (RS-485) outputs as standard
- 316 stainless steel electronics housing with a 316L measuring tube and sapphire windows
- Integrated process pressure sensor for pressure-compensated density and diagnostics
- Two-line backlit display with local totaliser and diagnostics
- Works in water with turbidity as low as 0.2 NTU

## Ordering information

Order codes follow the pattern LF60P-{size}-PN40, where the size is the three-digit nominal diameter. For example, LF60P-050-PN40 is the DN50 meter and LF60P-100-PN40 is the DN100 meter. Add the suffix -HC for Hastelloy C-276 wetted parts (for example LF60P-050-PN40-HC). Prices are published in the current regional price list.

## Typical applications

- Boiler feed water and condensate return up to 120 °C
- High-pressure water injection and reverse osmosis feed
- Process water in chemical and pharmaceutical plants
- Flooded valve chambers and outdoor pits where IP67 is required

## Installation notes

Install the LF-60P with at least 15 pipe diameters of straight pipe upstream and 5 downstream of the meter. The measuring tube must remain full; on vertical pipes, install with upward flow. Grounding rings are mandatory on non-conductive or lined pipe. Full installation, wiring, HART commissioning and flange bolting instructions are given in the Lumen LF-60P Installation Manual (prod-lf60p-installation-manual).

## Compliance

CE and UKCA marked. Pressure Equipment Directive 2014/68/EU, category I for DN25 to DN50, category II for DN65 to DN150. EMC to EN 61326-1 industrial environment. HART registered with FieldComm Group, device revision 3. The LF-60P is not certified for use in hazardous areas.
