## 3. Halcyon gas detection

Halcyon is our portable gas detector family for personal protection in oil and gas, water and wastewater, utilities and confined-space work. It consists of three near-identical siblings that share a housing design, user interface and docking station.

- HX-200: single-gas H2S detector, lightest in the family at 210 g, with a 36-hour runtime. Positioned for high-volume personal H2S monitoring. Firmware 3.2.4.
- HX-210: four-gas detector (O2, LEL, CO, H2S) with Zone 0 certification (Ex ia IIC T4 Ga). Our volume four-gas product. Firmware 3.4.1.
- HX-220: four-gas detector with Bluetooth LE, GPS and man-down alarm, 22-hour runtime and -40 °C operation for lone-worker programmes. Zone 1 certified. Firmware 3.4.2.

All Halcyon instruments are supported by the HX-DOCK-4 docking station for bump testing, calibration and data download. Halcyon hardware is assembled and calibrated in Penang, with sensor qualification in Leeds.

## 4. Tessera vibration monitoring

Tessera is our wireless condition monitoring platform for rotating equipment such as pumps, motors, fans and gearboxes.

- TS-4e edge node: battery-powered triaxial vibration and temperature sensor with a five-year battery life at one sample per hour. A Zone 2 variant, TS4E-NODE-3AX-Z2, is available.
- TS-4 gateway: collects data from up to 64 edge nodes over the TesseraMesh 2.4 GHz network and forwards it over Ethernet or LTE Cat-M1.
- TesseraCloud: the SaaS application for dashboards, alarming, trend analysis and the REST API, sold per monitored asset per year on Standard and Enterprise plans.

Tessera hardware is designed and built in Leeds. TesseraCloud is developed and operated by the platform engineering team in Austin.

## 5. Lumen optical flow

Lumen is our range of inline optical-correlation flow meters for clean and lightly loaded liquids.

- LF-60: PN16 meter for DN25 to DN200, ±1.0% of reading, IP65, Modbus RTU. Positioned for water utilities, building services and general industry.
- LF-60P: PN40 meter for DN25 to DN150, ±0.75% of reading, IP67, HART 7, fluid temperatures up to +120 °C. Positioned for process industry, boiler feed and high-pressure water.

Lumen meters are designed, manufactured and flow-calibrated in Rotterdam.

## 6. Portfolio priorities for FY2025/26

- Lumen: expand utility framework agreements in the UK, the Netherlands and Malaysia; complete HART 7 device revision 3 adoption on the LF-60P.
