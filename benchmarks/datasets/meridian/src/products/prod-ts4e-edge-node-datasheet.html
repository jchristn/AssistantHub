Tessera TS-4e Edge Node Datasheet

Document ID: prod-ts4e-edge-node-datasheet | Version: 3.1 | Effective: 2025-08-25 | Owner: Tessera Product Engineering

Overview

The Tessera TS-4e is a battery-powered wireless vibration and temperature sensor for rotating equipment such as pumps, fans, motors, compressors and gearboxes. Each node measures triaxial vibration with a MEMS accelerometer, computes condition indicators at the edge, and reports them over the TesseraMesh 2.4 GHz network to a TS-4 gateway, which forwards them to TesseraCloud. A single TS-4 gateway supports up to 64 TS-4e nodes.

Two variants are available. The standard node, TS4E-NODE-3AX, is for general industrial areas. The TS4E-NODE-3AX-Z2 variant is certified for Zone 2 hazardous areas and requires node firmware 2.8.0, which in turn requires gateway firmware 5.3.0.

Edge-computed indicators

  • Velocity RMS (10 Hz to 1 kHz), per axis, in mm/s, for ISO 10816-3 evaluation
  • Acceleration peak and RMS, per axis
  • Crest factor and kurtosis, for early bearing fault detection
  • Surface temperature
  • Optional waveform capture (up to 16,384 samples) on demand or on alarm

Battery life by reporting interval

Reporting intervalEstimated battery life
1 sample per hour (default)5 years
1 sample per 30 minutes3 years
1 sample per 10 minutes14 months
1 sample per hour plus daily waveform4 years

Estimates assume 20 C, a first-hop connection to the gateway and no relaying of other nodes' traffic. Nodes that relay for neighbours typically see 15 to 25 percent shorter battery life. The node reports a low-battery event, T-305, when the estimated remaining capacity falls below 10 percent.

Mounting guidance

  • Mount on the bearing housing, as close as possible to the load zone, with the Z axis radial.
  • Spot-face the mounting surface to a flat diameter of at least 30 mm and drill and tap M6 x 10 mm.
  • Screw the stud into the machine, then screw the node onto the stud and tighten to 6 N m.
  • Where drilling is not permitted, use the epoxy mounting pad TS4E-PAD-EPX; allow 24 hours cure before enabling alarms.
  • Magnetic mounting (TS4E-MAG-01) is supported for surveys only; it reduces usable frequency range to about 2 kHz.
