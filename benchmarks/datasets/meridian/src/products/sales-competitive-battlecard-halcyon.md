# Competitive Battlecard: Halcyon vs Corvane GX4 and Brightline BL-Quad

Document ID: sales-competitive-battlecard-halcyon | Version: 1 | Effective: 2025-07-10 | Owner: Sales Enablement

For internal use only. Do not forward to customers or partners.

## Who we meet

In portable four-gas deals we most often compete against two vendors:

- Corvane Safety, with the Corvane GX4. It is the price leader in UK utilities and Dutch contractors, and is usually sold through large safety distributors.
- Brightline Detection, with the BL-Quad. It is strong in North American oil and gas and is gaining ground in Malaysia through a regional distributor.

Our answer in these deals is the Halcyon HX-210 (Zone 0, four-gas) or the HX-220 (connected four-gas with Bluetooth LE, GPS and man-down). For single-gas H2S work, lead with the HX-200.

## Why we win

- Total cost of ownership. Our 36-month instrument warranty (with product registration within 90 days) and 24-month warranty on most sensors cut replacement spend. Under sup-warranty-terms-2025 the O2 sensor carries 18 months.
- Docking. HX-DOCK-4 runs automatic bump tests and calibration for four instruments at once, and records results for audit.
- Cold weather. The HX-220 works down to -40 C, well below both competitors.
- Connected safety. On lone-worker sites the HX-220 man-down alarm and GPS position replace a separate lone-worker device.

## Landmines to set

- "Ask them what their confined-space entry procedure says about Zone 0." Corvane GX4 is Zone 1 only.
- "How many days of datalog do you need for an incident investigation?" The GX4 keeps 30 days, the HX-220 keeps 180.
- "What does a replacement sensor cost after the warranty ends?" Brightline sensor pricing is typically 20 to 30 percent higher than ours.

## Critical guidance: Zone 0 work

The HX-220 is certified II 2G Ex ib IIC T4 Gb, which is Zone 1 only. It must not be proposed for Zone 0 areas such as the inside of tanks, vessels and sewers during entry. If the customer needs Zone 0, recommend the HX-210 (II 1G Ex ia IIC T4 Ga). If they also want connectivity, propose a mixed fleet: HX-210 for entrants and HX-220 for attendants and area patrols. Brightline will attack us on this point, because the BL-Quad is Zone 0 and has Bluetooth. Answer with runtime (HX-210 14 h against 12 h), our longer warranty and the dock.

The HX-200 is certified for gas group IIB only (Ex ib IIB T4 Gb). Do not propose it where hydrogen or acetylene may be present. Those gases fall in group IIC.

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Corvane is 15% cheaper." | Compare over three years. Include sensor replacements, the 24-month versus 36-month warranty, and calibration gas used. The HX-210's 120-day interval uses fewer MI-CAL-Q4-B cylinders than 90-day competitors. |
| "Brightline has Zone 0 and Bluetooth in one unit." | True. Position a mixed HX-210/HX-220 fleet and lead on the HX-220's 22 h runtime and -40 C rating for the patrol role. |
| "Your calibration interval went from 180 to 120 days." | The HX-210 calibration procedure v2 (May 2025) moved to 25% LEL span gas and 120 days after field drift data. Present it as a quality improvement. Brightline still needs 90 days. |
| "We already have Corvane docks." | Offer a trade-in on competitor docks through the deal desk. This counts toward the total discount under the approval matrix. |

## Discounting guidance

Keep competitive discounts within 20% where possible. Anything above 20% needs VP Sales approval under sales-discount-approval-matrix. Record the competitor's name in the Atlas opportunity so win/loss reporting stays accurate.
