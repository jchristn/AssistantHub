# Demo Kit Booking Procedure

Document ID: sales-demo-kit-booking | Version: 1 | Effective: 2025-02-14 | Owner: Sales Operations

## Purpose

Meridian keeps a pool of demonstration kits in each region for customer trials and trade events. This procedure covers booking, shipping and returning them. Kits are company assets tracked in Atlas and must never be invoiced, sold or left with a customer beyond the loan period.

| Kit ID | Contents | Held at |
|---|---|---|
| DK-HX-01 to DK-HX-06 | HX-210 and HX-220 instruments, HX-DOCK-4, MI-CAL-Q4-B cylinder | Leeds (01-03), Rotterdam (04), Austin (05), Penang (06) |

There is no LF-60P demonstration kit. For LF-60P trials, use a witnessed test at the Leeds flow rig, arranged through Product Management.

## Booking

1. Check availability in the Atlas demo calendar (Sales > Demo Equipment > Calendar).
2. Create a demo loan order in Atlas with order type ZDL. Link it to the customer opportunity and enter the ship-to address, loan start date and return date.
3. The maximum loan period is 21 days from delivery. Extensions up to a total of 35 days need regional sales manager approval, recorded on the ZDL order. Longer trials must be converted to a paid evaluation.
4. Book at least 5 working days before the required delivery date. Cross-region loans, for example an Austin kit shipped to Mexico, need 10 working days for export paperwork.

## Shipping

Kits with calibration gas cylinders are dangerous goods (UN1956, compressed gas). Logistics ships them by road only. For air shipments, the cylinder is removed and the customer supplies local gas. Send the customer the relevant quick-start guide with the kit.

## Return and inspection

When the kit comes back, the regional logistics team inspects it within 3 working days:

- Count every item against the kit checklist
- Bump test and calibrate Halcyon instruments in the dock
- Check Tessera node batteries and reset the demo tenant
- Record any missing or damaged items on the ZDL order

Missing items are charged to the opportunity owner's cost centre unless the customer has agreed to buy them. Kits returned more than 7 days late are flagged in the monthly sales operations report.
