# Sales Discount Approval Matrix

Document ID: sales-discount-approval-matrix | Version: 1 | Effective: 2025-04-01 | Owner: Sales Operations

## Purpose

This matrix says who may approve a discount off list price on a Meridian quotation. It applies to all product lines (Halcyon, Tessera, Lumen), TesseraCloud subscriptions and support contracts, in all four sales regions.

## How total discount is calculated

The discount level is the total effective discount off the published list price for the whole quotation. Standard volume breaks, multi-site support discounts and any negotiated discount all count toward it. Partner programme discounts for authorised resellers are handled separately under the Meridian Partner Programme Guide and do not count toward the total, unless the partner asks for extra project pricing on top.

## Approval bands

| Total discount off list | Approver | Typical turnaround |
|---|---|---|
| 0-10% | Account manager (self-approve) | Immediate |
| More than 10% up to 20% | Regional sales manager | 1 working day |
| More than 20% up to 30% | VP Sales | 2 working days |
| More than 30% | CFO, with margin review by VP Product (Samuel Achterberg) | 5 working days |

## Deal desk

Any opportunity with a total contract value above GBP 250,000, or the equivalent in EUR, USD or MYR at the Atlas planning rate, must go through the deal desk whatever the discount level. The deal desk meets on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Submissions must be in Atlas by 12:00 UK time on the previous working day.

The deal desk also handles:
- Non-standard payment terms longer than 60 days
- Multi-year TesseraCloud commitments with price holds
- Quantities of 250 or more on one order line
- Any request for Zone 2 Tessera nodes (TS4E-NODE-3AX-Z2)

## Recording approvals

Every approval must be recorded in Atlas on the quotation's Discount Approval tab before the quotation is sent. Approvals in email or chat are not valid. Finance will not release an order where the discount on the order exceeds the approved level on the quotation. An approval stays valid for the quotation validity period, which defaults to 60 days. When a quotation is revised, the revision needs fresh approval if the total discount rises.

## Examples

- 25 units of HX-210-KIT-03 with a further 4% negotiated discount: 5% volume break + 4% = 9%. The account manager approves.
- Premier support for four sites (7.5% multi-site discount) plus a 5% goodwill discount: 12.5%. The regional sales manager approves.
- A Tessera rollout at GBP 310,000 total contract value and 8% discount: deal desk review is required because the value is over GBP 250,000, even though 8% is within account manager authority.
