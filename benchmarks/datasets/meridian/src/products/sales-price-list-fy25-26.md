# Hardware and Subscription Price List FY2025/26

Document ID: sales-price-list-fy25-26 | Version: 1 | Effective: 2025-04-01 | Owner: Sales Operations

## 1. Scope and validity

This price list sets the standard list prices for Meridian Instruments Ltd hardware, consumables and TesseraCloud subscriptions for fiscal year 2025/26. Prices are valid for orders entered in Atlas from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026 inclusive. Quotations issued before 1 April 2025 on the FY2024/25 price list may be honoured until their stated expiry date, and never later than 30 June 2025.

Four price columns are published: GBP for the United Kingdom and rest-of-world orders invoiced from Leeds, EUR for orders invoiced from Rotterdam, USD for orders invoiced from Austin, and MYR for orders invoiced from Penang. Account managers must quote in the currency of the invoicing entity. Do not convert between columns with a spot rate. The columns are set separately each year and reflect local duty, logistics and channel costs.

Prices for support contracts (Essential, Professional and Premier) are not shown here. They are published separately in the Support Contract Price List FY2025/26 (sales-support-contract-price-list).

## 3. SKU notes

### Halcyon
- HX-200-KIT-01 contains the HX-200 instrument with HX-BAT-200 battery, a USB-C charger, a calibration cap and a quick-start guide. Docking the HX-200 in an HX-DOCK-4 needs the HX-ADP-200 adapter, which is quoted separately.
- HX-210-KIT-03 contains the HX-210 instrument with HX-BAT-210 battery, a charger, calibration cap, belt clip and 1 m calibration hose.
- HX-220-KIT-05 contains the HX-220 instrument with HX-BAT-220L extended battery, a charger, a calibration cap, a hard carry case and a Meridian Link mobile app licence. The optional HX-PMP-22 sampling pump is HX-220 only.
- MI-CAL-Q4-B replaced MI-CAL-Q4-A, which was discontinued during 2025. Do not quote MI-CAL-Q4-A. The HX-210 calibration procedure v2 requires the B mixture.

### Tessera
- Every TS-4e edge node reports through a TS-4 gateway. One gateway supports up to 64 nodes.
- TesseraCloud subscriptions are billed per monitored asset per year, not per node. An asset with three nodes is billed as one asset.
- The Zone 2 edge node variant TS4E-NODE-3AX-Z2 is quoted on request through the deal desk and is not on this list.

### Lumen
- Only the DN50 variants are listed. Other bore sizes (LF-60 DN25 to DN200, LF-60P DN25 to DN150) are priced through the Lumen configurator in Atlas.

## 4. Volume breaks

Volume breaks apply to a single order line of one SKU shipped to one delivery address:

| Quantity per order line | Discount off list |
|---|---|
| 1-24 | 0% |
| 25-99 | 5% |
| 100-249 | 8% |
| 250 and above | Deal desk pricing |

Volume breaks do not apply to TC-SUB-STD, TC-SUB-ENT or calibration gas. They count toward the total discount when the approval level is decided under the Sales Discount Approval Matrix (sales-discount-approval-matrix). For example, a 5% volume break plus a 7% negotiated discount counts as 12% and needs regional sales manager approval.

## 5. Commercial terms

- Incoterms: hardware ships FCA from the invoicing site (Leeds, Rotterdam, Austin or Penang). DAP can be quoted, with freight added at cost plus 10%.
- Payment terms: 30 days net from invoice for established accounts. New accounts pay in advance until credit is approved in Atlas.
- Minimum order value: GBP 250 / EUR 290 / USD 315 / MYR 1,450, excluding subscriptions.
- Lead times: Halcyon and Tessera from stock, typically 5 working days. Lumen is built to order, typically 4 to 6 weeks.
- Subscriptions are invoiced annually in advance, and co-terming to the customer's renewal date is prorated monthly.
- Warranty terms are set by the current warranty terms document (sup-warranty-terms-2025) and are not modified by this price list.
