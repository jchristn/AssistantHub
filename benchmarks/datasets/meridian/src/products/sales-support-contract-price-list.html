Support Contract Price List FY2025/26

Document ID: sales-support-contract-price-list | Version: 1 | Effective: 2025-04-01 | Owner: Sales Operations

1. Purpose

This list sets the annual list prices for Meridian customer support contracts from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026. Hardware, consumables and TesseraCloud subscriptions are priced in the Hardware and Subscription Price List FY2025/26 (sales-price-list-fy25-26).

This list does not restate response times, coverage hours, uptime commitments or tier inclusions. The document "Customer Support SLA Tiers" (sup-sla-tiers) defines them and is the only authoritative source. When a customer asks what a tier includes, send them that document.

2. Annual price per site

Contracts are priced per customer site per year. A site is one physical location with one delivery address, whatever the number of Halcyon, Tessera or Lumen devices installed there. Prices exclude VAT, GST and sales tax.

Mixed-tier contracts, such as Premier at a refinery and Professional at two depots, count all sites toward the band. The discount applies to each tier's price separately. Multi-site discounts count toward the total discount under the Sales Discount Approval Matrix (sales-discount-approval-matrix).

4. Contract rules

  • Minimum term is 12 months. Multi-year terms of 24 or 36 months are quoted at the FY2025/26 price for every year when invoiced annually. There is no additional discount for multi-year terms without deal desk approval.
  • A tier upgrade mid-term is prorated monthly and takes effect on the first day of the next month. Downgrades take effect only at renewal.
  • Support contracts can only be sold for sites whose installed equipment is within warranty or has passed a Meridian health check. The health check is quoted at the field service day rate.
  • Premier contracts include a named Technical Account Manager. Before quoting Premier in Malaysia, confirm TAM availability with the Penang support lead.
  • TesseraCloud uptime service credits are paid under the SLA tier definitions, not under this price list.

5. Ordering

Enter support contracts in Atlas with the contract SKU and the site ID. Atlas creates the entitlement in the Meridian Support Centre (MSC) portal within one working day of order confirmation. Until then, tickets raised by the customer default to Essential handling.
