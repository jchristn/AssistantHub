# Field Service Visit Rates

Document ID: sup-field-service-rates | Version: 1 | Effective: 2025-04-01 | Owner: Customer Support (Rosa Delgado)

## Scope

This rate card applies to chargeable on-site work by Meridian field service engineers for fiscal year 2025/26 (1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026). It covers commissioning, repairs outside warranty, additional preventive visits, training on site, and visits requested by customers without a support contract. The number of preventive visits already included in a contract depends on the customer's support tier (Essential, Professional or Premier), as defined in Customer Support SLA Tiers (sup-sla-tiers). Visits beyond that allowance are charged at the rates below.

## Day rates

A day is up to 8 hours on site for one engineer. Half-day rates apply to visits of up to 4 hours on site.

| Rate | GBP | EUR | USD | MYR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard day rate (Mon-Fri) | 1,150 | 1,335 | 1,450 | 6,650 |
| Half day (Mon-Fri) | 650 | 755 | 820 | 3,760 |
| Saturday day rate | 1,610 | 1,870 | 2,030 | 9,310 |
| Sunday and public holiday day rate | 2,300 | 2,670 | 2,900 | 13,300 |
| Emergency call-out surcharge (within 24 hours of request) | 450 | 520 | 565 | 2,600 |
| Additional hour beyond 8 hours | 175 | 205 | 220 | 1,010 |

Rates are charged in the currency of the invoicing Meridian entity: GBP from Leeds, EUR from Rotterdam, USD from Austin and MYR from Penang.

## Travel

- Travel time is charged at 50% of the hourly equivalent of the standard day rate, starting from the nearest regional hub.
- Mileage within 100 km of the hub is included. Beyond 100 km, mileage is charged at GBP 0.65, EUR 0.75, USD 0.80 or MYR 3.70 per km.
- Flights, rail and hotels are recharged at cost plus 10%. Overnight stays are needed when the site is more than 250 km from the hub.
- Offshore and remote-site visits need mandatory safety training certificates (for example OPITO BOSIET) and are quoted individually.

## What is included

- Engineer time on site and standard tools.
- Calibration gas for up to 10 Halcyon instruments per day (MI-CAL-H2S25 or MI-CAL-Q4-B).
- A written service report posted to the linked MSC ticket within 3 business days.

Spare parts, replacement sensors and replacement units are charged at list price from the current price list (sales-price-list-fy25-26) unless covered by warranty.

## Booking and cancellation

Visits are booked through an MSC ticket at least 10 business days in advance, except emergency call-outs. Cancellations less than 3 business days before the visit are charged at 50% of the day rate; cancellations on the day, or where the engineer cannot get access to the site, are charged in full.
