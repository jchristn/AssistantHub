# Customer Support SLA Tiers

Document ID: sup-sla-tiers | Version: 1 | Effective: 2025-04-01 | Owner: Customer Support (Rosa Delgado)

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the three customer support service levels offered by Meridian Instruments Ltd for the Halcyon, Tessera and Lumen product lines: Essential, Professional and Premier. It sets out coverage hours, response targets by ticket priority, TesseraCloud availability commitments, service credits and included on-site services. Ticket priorities P1 to P4 are defined in the Support Ticket Priority Codes document (sup-ticket-priority-codes). Annual contract prices for each tier are published in the Support Contract Price List (sales-support-contract-price-list).

These terms apply to all support contracts signed or renewed on or after 1 April 2025, the start of fiscal year 2025/26.

## 2. Service levels at a glance

| Service element | Essential | Professional | Premier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coverage hours | Mon-Fri 08:00-18:00 local time | Mon-Fri 07:00-20:00 local time | 24x7x365 |
| Contact channels | MSC portal, email | MSC portal, email, phone | MSC portal, email, phone, dedicated P1 hotline |
| P1 Critical response | 8 business hours | 4 hours | 1 hour |
| P2 High response | 2 business days | 8 business hours | 4 hours |
| P3 Medium response | 5 business days | 2 business days | 1 business day |
| P4 Low response | Best effort | 5 business days | 3 business days |
| TesseraCloud monthly uptime | 99.5% | 99.7% | 99.9% |
| Advance replacement | No | Yes | Yes, next business day |
| Preventive on-site visits per year | None (chargeable) | 2 | 4 |
| Named Technical Account Manager | No | No | Yes |
| Quarterly service review | No | No | Yes |
| Firmware upgrade assistance | Self-service | Remote assisted | Remote or on-site assisted |

Response time is measured from the moment a ticket is logged in the Meridian Support Centre (MSC) portal, or from the moment a phone call is answered, to the first substantive response by a qualified support engineer. An automated acknowledgement does not count as a response. Business hours and business days follow the calendar of the customer's regional support hub (Leeds, Rotterdam, Austin or Penang).

## 3. Resolution targets

Meridian does not guarantee resolution times, because many faults depend on site access, gas supply or third-party networks. We do publish target restoration times for P1 tickets: 2 business days for Essential, 1 business day for Professional and 8 hours for Premier. For hardware faults the target is met when a working replacement unit is dispatched under the RMA process.

## 4. TesseraCloud availability and service credits

TesseraCloud availability is measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 5 business days in advance and capped at 6 hours per month. If monthly availability falls below the committed level, the customer may claim a service credit against the next TesseraCloud subscription invoice.

| Monthly availability shortfall | Essential credit | Professional credit | Premier credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to 0.5 percentage points below commitment | 5% | 10% | 10% |
| 0.5 to 1.0 percentage points below commitment | 10% | 15% | 20% |
| More than 1.0 percentage points below commitment | 15% | 25% | 30% |

Credits are calculated on the monthly TesseraCloud subscription fee for the affected tenant only, and must be claimed through the MSC portal within 30 days of the end of the affected month. Total credits in any month may not exceed 30% of that month's subscription fee. Service credits are the sole remedy for availability shortfalls.

## 5. Included on-site services

Professional contracts include 2 preventive on-site visits per year per contracted site; Premier contracts include 4. A preventive visit is one engineer for up to one working day and typically covers Halcyon docking-station checks and bump-test audits, TS-4 gateway health checks and TS-4e node battery surveys, or Lumen zero-point verification. Unused visits do not carry over into the next contract year. Additional visits are charged at the rates in the Field Service Visit Rates document (sup-field-service-rates).

Premier customers also receive a P1 on-site commitment: where a P1 fault cannot be resolved remotely, an engineer will be on site within 48 hours in the UK, the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, the continental United States and Peninsular Malaysia, and within 5 business days elsewhere.

## 6. Technical Account Manager

Premier customers are assigned a named Technical Account Manager (TAM) from the regional hub. The TAM owns the quarterly service review, tracks open P1 and P2 tickets, coordinates firmware roll-out plans and acts as the escalation point before the regional support manager. Professional and Essential customers are served by the pooled support team.

## 7. Exclusions

SLA commitments do not apply to issues caused by unsupported firmware combinations (see prod-tessera-firmware-compatibility-matrix), use of calibration gas outside the published specification, products outside warranty and without a support contract, customer network outages, or force majeure events. Response targets are suspended while a ticket is in the "Awaiting customer" state.

## 8. Changing tier

Customers may upgrade tier at any time; the upgrade applies from the next business day and is charged pro rata. Downgrades take effect only at contract renewal.
