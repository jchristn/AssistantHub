# Meridian Limited Warranty Terms 2025

Document ID: sup-warranty-terms-2025 | Version: 2 | Effective: 2025-04-01 | Supersedes: sup-warranty-terms-2024 | Owner: Customer Support (Rosa Delgado), with Legal

## 1. Application

These limited warranty terms apply to Halcyon, Tessera and Lumen products shipped by Meridian Instruments Ltd, Meridian Instruments B.V., Meridian Instruments Inc. and Meridian Instruments Sdn Bhd on or after 1 April 2025, the start of fiscal year 2025/26. Products shipped before that date remain covered by the 2024 terms. The warranty period starts on the date of shipment from Meridian, or the date of delivery to the end customer when a partner can evidence it, whichever is later.

## 2. Product registration

To receive the 36-month warranty periods shown below, the product must be registered in the Meridian Support Centre (MSC) portal within 90 days of the warranty start date. Registration requires the serial number and the end customer's site details. Products that are not registered within 90 days receive a 24-month warranty in place of any 36-month period. All other periods are unaffected by registration.

## 3. What is covered

Meridian warrants that each product will be free from defects in materials and workmanship, and will perform substantially in accordance with its published specification, for the period shown below when installed, operated and maintained in accordance with its user manual.

| Product or component | Warranty period |
|---|---|
| Halcyon instruments (HX-200, HX-210, HX-220) | 36 months (registration required) |
| Halcyon sensors HX-SNS-H2S-02, HX-SNS-CO-03, HX-SNS-LEL-04 | 24 months |
| Halcyon oxygen sensor HX-SNS-O2-01 | 18 months |
| Halcyon batteries (HX-BAT-200, HX-BAT-210, HX-BAT-220L) | 12 months |
| HX-DOCK-4 docking station | 24 months |
| Tessera TS-4 gateway (TS4-GW-LTE, TS4-GW-ETH) | 36 months (registration required) |
| Tessera TS-4e edge node (TS4E-NODE-3AX and TS4E-NODE-3AX-Z2) | 24 months |
| Lumen flow meters (LF-60, LF-60P) | 36 months |
| LF-60P wetted parts (measuring tube, electrodes, liner) | 60 months |
| Spare parts and accessories sold separately | 6 months |

Replacement parts or units supplied under warranty are warranted for the remainder of the original warranty period or 90 days, whichever is longer.

## 4. Remedy

Meridian will, at its option, repair or replace a defective product or refund the purchase price. Warranty service is provided through the Return Material Authorisation process (sup-rma-process-v2), including the mandatory decontamination declaration MI-F-031. Firmware defects are remedied by a firmware update.

## 5. Making a claim

A warranty claim must be raised as an MSC ticket within 30 days of the customer discovering the defect. The ticket must identify the product serial number and describe the defect, including any error code shown.

## 6. Exclusions

This warranty does not cover:

- Consumables, including calibration gas cylinders (MI-CAL-H2S25, MI-CAL-Q4-B), filters, and sensor caps.
- Sensor poisoning or inhibition caused by exposure to silicones, lead compounds, sulphur compounds above rated levels, or high concentrations of the target gas beyond the sensor range. An E-117 poisoned-sensor code is treated as poisoning unless teardown shows a manufacturing defect.
- Damage caused by incorrect installation, including Lumen meters installed without the required straight pipe run or with incorrect flange bolt torque.
- Damage caused by use outside the rated temperature, pressure or ingress protection.
- Products that have been opened, modified or repaired by anyone other than Meridian or an authorised service partner, other than routine sensor and battery replacement described in the user manual.
- Normal wear, cosmetic damage, and damage in transit where the product was not packed as instructed.
- Faults caused by running unsupported firmware combinations.
- TesseraCloud subscriptions, which are governed by the TesseraCloud subscription terms and the SLA in sup-sla-tiers.

## 7. Calibration

Halcyon warranty cover requires the instrument to have been calibrated at the interval stated in its current calibration procedure. For the HX-210 this is 120 days under procedure prod-hx210-calibration-procedure-v2. Meridian may request calibration records, including HX-DOCK-4 logs, in support of a claim involving sensor performance.

## 8. Limitation

To the extent permitted by law, the remedies above are the customer's sole remedies for defective products, and Meridian's liability is limited to the price paid for the defective product. Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or any liability that cannot be limited by law. Statutory rights of consumers are not affected.
